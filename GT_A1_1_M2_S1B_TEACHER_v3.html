§ 01
Picture Prompt — What does this person do?
🖼️ Choose a photo · Describe the work routine using Simple Present · 4–5 sentences · Speak from the first second
⏱ 5–7 MIN
🚫 S1B — NO new grammar · NO reading · 100% oral production · S1A Simple Present freely recycled
⚡
Quick Win — 30 seconds · You can do it!
Without looking at notes: say 3 things you do every day at work using Simple Present.
"I check email. I have meetings. I don't work on weekends." — from memory. You already know it! 🎯
👀 Choose a photo. Look at the person. Say 4–5 oraciones sobre su rutina laboral diaria. ¿Qué hace? ¿Qué no hace? ¿A qué hora empieza o termina? Usa "I/you/he/she" + verbo. No hay respuesta incorrecta — ¡cualquier oración vale!

Usa: "He/She starts at ___." · "He/She checks ___." · "He/She does not ___." · "I do not ___ on weekends."
Scaffold visible al student (si lo necesita):
"He/She starts at ___."  ·  "He/She checks his/her email."  ·  "He/She sends ___."  ·  "He/She does not ___."  ·  "He/She leaves at ___."
Teacher Follow-Up — Push for More
"Does he/she work on weekends? Yes or no?"
"What time does he/she leave? Make up a time!"
"Does she eat at her desk or in the lunch room?"
"Now describe Foto 2 also — just 2 sentences."
Layer 1 · Pedagogical
  • ACTION: Student must produce at least 1 affirmative, 1 negative (don't/doesn't) and 1 question (Do/Does…?) about the photo.
  • S1B starts warmer than S1A. The student already knows the verbs and structure. If they hesitate: "You know this. Last class you learned it. Any sentence is a win."
  • Do not correct in the moment. Listen and note down don't/doesn't errors. Correct them in §3 Audio Model.
  • Photo 3 (video call) allows using "Do you mute your mic?" / "Does she share her screen?" — connects with GT Originals from S1A.
Layer 2 · Engagement
  • "Last time you needed the grammar table. Today you are doing it from memory. That IS progress. That IS your English." 📈
Layer 3 · Retención
  • "In 50 minutes you will describe YOUR complete work routine in English — without notes, without aids. This picture prompt is the warm-up." 🎯

§ 03
Audio Model — Listen and Repeat
🎧 Ana describes her complete routine · ElevenLabs model · Shadowing phrase by phrase · Then you describe your routine like Ana
⏱ 8–10 MIN
🎙️ Audio Model — Ana García (complete routine)
ElevenLabs · speed 0.78 · Listen completely first · Then shadowing phrase by phrase ↓

🔊 Escucha cada frase → repite out loud → escucha de nuevo. Pon atención al "don't" y al "do not". 3 rondas mínimo por frase.

🔊 Shadowing — 3 Key Phrases from S1A
The same 3 phrases you learned in S1A. Now you say them fluently, without the table.
1
"I arrive at the office at eight in the morning."
2
"I do not eat at my desk."
3
"I arrive on time every day."
🎙️ Now You — Mismo Formato que Ana
Describe your own work routine in 4–5 sentences — just as Ana described hers. Include: arrival time, 2 things you do, 1 thing you don't do, departure time.
➡ "I usually start at ___. First, I check/send ___. I have ___. I do not ___. I leave at ___."
Layer 1 · Pedagogical
  • ACTION: Shadowing — student says the phrase BEFORE the audio, then verifies with 🔊. Not the other way around.
  • Phrase 2 ("I do not eat at my desk") — emphasize "do not" vs "don't". Both are correct. "do not" is more formal but clarifies there is NO -s on "eat".
  • In "Now You" — if the student says "I don't works" → immediate recast: "I don't WORK — do you hear the difference?"
  • "Now You" is the direct warm-up for the §4 role play. If it goes well here → the role play will flow.
Layer 2 · Engagement
  • "You just described your work routine exactly as Ana would when talking with her manager in the U.S. That is already real work English." 💼
Layer 3 · Retención
  • "In the §4 role play you will do the same thing — but live, without a model, with real questions. The audio was the general rehearsal." 🎭
§ 04
Role Play — My Work Day
🎭 Main S1B activity · 3 progressive rounds: guided → semi-free → free · Describe your real workday to an English-speaking colleague
⏱ 15–18 MIN
🎭 Round 1 — Guided (scaffold visible)
📞 "Tell me about your typical work day"
You have an onboarding call with your new American colleague. They want to know about your workday. The scaffold is available.
👔 Teacher (new U.S. colleague)
Mike Johnson · Chicago
Start: "Hi! I want to learn about your typical work day. What time do you usually start? Do you eat at your desk?"
🗣️ Student (you)
Your name · Celaya
Describe your real routine: arrival time, what you do first, lunch, departure time. Minimum 4 sentences. Include 1 negative with "don't".
Scaffold Round 1 (visible):
"I usually start at ___. First, I check/send ___."
"I have a meeting ___ at ___. I eat lunch at ___."
"I do not eat at my desk. I leave at ___."
"Do I work on weekends? No, I don't."
🎭 Round 2 — Semi-libre (solo prompts del teacher)
📞 Segunda llamada — sin scaffold
Misma situación. Ahora sin scaffold. El teacher hace preguntas que guían la conversación.
👔 Teacher — available questions
Scaffolding prompts
"What time do you start?" · "Do you have meetings?" · "Do you eat at your desk?" · "What time do you leave?" · "Do you work on weekends?"
🗣️ Student — without scaffold
Just respond
Respond with complete sentences. Short answers + expansion: "No, I don't. I eat in the lunch room at two."
⭐ Round 3 — Libre (Challenge: 60 segundos sin parar)
🎤 Full Routine Report — Solo performance
Sin teacher como interlocutor. El student habla solo durante 60 segundos describiendo su rutina completa. El teacher cronometra y cuenta los verbos usados correctamente.
🏆 Objetivo: 6+ verbos correctos en 60 segundos = ⭐ Excelente · 4–5 = ✅ Bien · 1–3 = 📋 Practica en casa
Sample Answer — Rutina laboral completa:
"I usually start work at eight thirty. First, I check my email and messages. I have a team meeting every Monday at nine. After the meeting, I send a daily report to my manager. I eat lunch at two — I don't eat at my desk. I leave the office at six. I don't work on weekends. I'm not a morning person, but I always arrive on time!"
Layer 1 · Pedagogical
  • ACTION: R1 — scaffold visible. R2 — only your questions. R3 — timer, no interruptions, count verbs.
  • En R1 y R2: recast de "don't works" → "don't work". En R3: NO interrumpas — solo cuenta verbos correctos.
  • If the student gets stuck in R2 → give them a closed question: "Do you eat at your desk? Yes or no?" One question is enough to reactivate.
  • R3 es el momento de mayor impacto emocional. Si llega a 6+ verbos → celebrate específicamente: "Usaste 7 verbos correctos en 60 segundos. Eso es inglés laboral funcional."
Layer 2 · Engagement
  • "Acabas de describir tu semana a un colega en Chicago. En inglés. Without notes. Si te preguntaran esto en una llamada real mañana — ya puedes responder." 📞
Layer 3 · Retención
  • "En S2A agregas 'she manages, he doesn't travel' — 3ª persona. Hoy tienes la base. El siguiente paso la multiplica." 📈
§ 05
Guessing Game — "¿Qué hago?"
🎮 S1B WOW activity · Describe an activity without saying the VERB · Teacher guesses the verb · Simple Present in real context
⏱ 8–10 MIN
🎮 Guessing Game — "¿Qué hago?"
Describe la actividad SIN decir el verbo. El teacher adivina. 3 rondas + 1 ronda inversa.
Cómo se juega — Ronda del student
Piensa en una de las 10 acciones de S1A (start, finish, arrive, leave, work, check, send, have, go, eat). Descríbela en 2–3 oraciones SIN usar el verbo. El teacher tiene 3 preguntas de Sí/No para adivinarlo.
💡 Ejemplo: "I do this every morning. I do it with my computer. I look at new messages." → El teacher adivina: "Do you check your email?" → Alumno: "Yes, I do!"
Ejemplos de descripciones without lookingbo
"I do this at 8 AM. I come to the building. I say hello to the receptionist." → arrive
"I do this with my phone or computer. I write words to my manager." → send
"I do this with a group of people. We discuss the week. It is usually on Monday." → have (a meeting)
"I do this at 6 PM. I go home. I say goodbye." → leave
🔄 Ronda Inversa — the teacher describe, the student adivina
El teacher describe una acción de S1A sin usar el verbo. El student hace preguntas de Sí/No para adivinar. Luego forma una oración completa con el verbo correcto.
💡 Maestro: "I do this at 2 PM. I go to a different room. I don't do it at my desk." → Alumno pregunta: "Do you eat?" → Maestro: "Yes, I do!" → Alumno forma: "Ana eats lunch at two."
Layer 1 · Pedagogical
  • ACTION: Student describes the action in complete sentences. Just "computer" is not enough — they need "I do this with my computer."
  • Teacher asks Yes/No questions → student practices short answers "Yes, I do. / No, I don't." in real context, not mechanical.
  • Reverse round: when the teacher describes → student practices forming "Do you…?" questions with S1A verbs. Double practice.
  • If the student uses "I make" instead of "I have" (a meeting) → it is a real recurring error. Soft recast: "I HAVE a meeting. But good try!"
Layer 2 · Engagement
  • "¿Ves? Acabas de hablar de tu rutina laboral en inglés sin pensar en gramática. Eso es lo que pasa cuando el idioma es tuyo." 🎯
Layer 3 · Retención
  • "En S2A el mismo juego agrega 'He sends / She arrives' — 3ª persona. Hoy tienes los verbos. El siguiente paso cambia el sujeto." 🔄
§ 06
Debate — Práctica Oral Libre
🗣️ 3 questions about real work routine · Free Simple Present · Personal opinion and experience · No required structure
⏱ 8–10 MIN
💬 Pregunta 1 — Tu rutina ideal
What is your ideal work day? What time do you start? What do you do first? Do you eat at your desk or in a lunch room?
➡ Usa: "In my ideal day, I start at ___. First I ___. I do not ___. I leave at ___."
💬 Pregunta 2 — ¿Trabajas o no trabajas?
Do you work on weekends? Do you check work email at home? Do you answer messages after 6 PM? What do you think about this?
➡ Usa: "Yes, I sometimes ___. / No, I never ___. / I think ___. / I do not ___ because ___."
⭐ Pregunta 3 — Challenge
Describe the routine of someone you work with — your manager or a colleague. What does he/she do? What does he/she NOT do? At least 4 sentences.
➡ Libre — oraciones con he/she + verbo (¡atención: en S1A no tienen la -s todavía — acepta formas sin -s en S1B!)
Layer 1 · Pedagogical
  • ACTION: Minimal correction. Soft recast only for serious errors. Let the student flow.
  • Question 3 is strategic: if the student says "She arrive" without -s → do NOT correct now. That is exactly the gap that S2A will close. Note it mentally.
  • Pregunta 2 abre conversación cultural rica — rutinas de trabajo México vs. EE.UU. Permite que divague un poco: eso es producción oral real.
Layer 2 · Engagement
  • "Estás dando tu opinión sobre el trabajo en inglés. No estás traduciendo — estás pensando en inglés. Eso es diferente." 🧠
Layer 3 · Retención
  • "En S2A agregas la -s para he/she: 'She arrives, he checks, she sends.' Notaste en la Pregunta 3 que la necesitas. S2A la da." 🔑
