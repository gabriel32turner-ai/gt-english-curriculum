LIS-05 Predict and Score: before each pause, bet on what the speaker will say. One point per correct prediction.

HOST (US English): Jun moved to a new city eleven months ago for work. He is thirty-four.

HOST: Every night, before he sleeps, he talks to an app. His sister, Ifeoma, found out last month.

HOST: We asked both of them the same question: is this helping?

[PAUSE 1]: Predict: what will Jun say? Write one sentence.

JUN (Korean English): I know how it sounds. But at two in the morning there is nobody to call.

JUN: The app does not get tired of me. It does not change the subject. It does not bring it up a week later.

JUN: I can open up to it about work, about my father, about money, and nothing comes back at me.

JUN: Is it the real thing? No. Does it fill a void? Yes, and I am not going to pretend it doesn't.

[PAUSE 2]: Predict: what will his sister say? Write one sentence.

IFEOMA (Nigerian English): He says nobody is awake at two in the morning. I am awake. I am six hours ahead, Jun.

IFEOMA: That is the part that hurts. Before the app, he used to call me on Sundays and talk for an hour.

IFEOMA: Now he calls for ten minutes and says everything is fine.

IFEOMA: I am not angry about the app. I am worried that it is easier than me, and that easy is going to win.

Answer key:

  • Jun's reasons: nobody to call at 2 a.m. · the app does not get tired or change the subject · nothing comes back at him later · it fills a void but it is not the real thing.
  • Ifeoma's reasons: she is awake, six hours ahead · the Sunday call went from an hour to ten minutes · she is not angry about the app, she is worried that easy wins.
  • Scoring: one point for each prediction that names a reason the speaker actually gives. Wording does not have to match.

    Goes with: it grows on you

    Respuestas modelo

    • Para en el audio en las dos marcas [PAUSE] y deja 30 segundos para escribir la predicción. Sin la pausa, LIS-05 deja de ser apuesta y se vuelve comprensión normal.
    • Evidencia modelo: “I take the point that 63% said it helped, and I'm not going to argue with that. Even so, 22% talk less to friends since. It may well give him companionship for a year. But chances are he'll be having the same ten-minute Sunday call in five years, and that is a substitute, not support.”
    • Las razones exactas de Jun y de Ifeoma están en la hoja de respuestas del bloque de input.

    Errores esperables en B2.1

    • Mezclar grados de certeza: “It's bound to maybe happen”.
    • Usar futuro simple donde el sentido pide continuo: “next year he will talk every night” → “will be talking”.
    • Saltarse la concesión en la ronda 3, que es la única que puntúa.
    • Tratar los números de la encuesta como estudio real. Están declarados como datos inventados en la tarjeta.
    • Convertir el debate en “la tecnología es mala”. El tema es este caso, no la tecnología.

    Si el grupo va adelantado o atrasado

    Adelantado: Pide una ronda 4 en la que cada quien defienda la postura de su oponente usando la evidencia que el otro trajo.

    Atrasado: Dos rondas en vez de tres y reproduce el audio una segunda vez completo antes de la ronda con evidencia.

    Dinámicas y límite del nivel

    LIS-05 Predict and Score · VOC-10 Sixty-Second Sell · GRM-12 Same Fact, Three Frames · SPK-05 Escalation Debate (central). Primera Spicy del nivel.

    No pedir todavía: deducción en pasado (must have), wish / if only, condicionales mixtos, verbos de reporte formales, negociación y conducción de juntas.

    Riesgo corporativo

    Spicy, seguro en tercera persona. A nadie se le pregunta si usa estas apps. Si la soledad se vuelve personal, el maestro agradece y regresa al caso de Jun. Nunca se pide a un alumno que hable de su propia soledad.
