MAYA (Indian English): We were close for eight years. Then last spring she just went silent. Three months, nothing.

MAYA: I called twice, I sent a message, and nothing came back. The thing that hurt was my birthday dinner.

MAYA: I booked a table for nine, and her chair stayed empty all night. People asked where she was and I had no answer.

MAYA: So yes, in the end I cut her off. I kept putting up with it and I got tired.

MAYA: You reach a point where you stop giving someone the benefit of the doubt.

DANA (Brazilian English): That is not how I remember it. It was five weeks, not three months, and I was at that dinner.

DANA: I arrived late, I sat down, I ate, and I left early because I was not well. She was talking to other people, so maybe she did not see me.

DANA: And her message — I answered it the next morning. I still have it on my phone.

DANA: Look, I was going through a rough patch. My father was in hospital and I could not explain that in a text.

DANA: I was not being toxic. I was not being anything. I just had nothing left to give that month.

DANA: She says I disappeared. I say I was drowning and nobody noticed.

\n

Answer key:

  • How long the silence lasted — Maya: three months. Dana: five weeks.
  • The birthday dinner — Maya: the chair stayed empty all night. Dana: she arrived late, ate, and left early.
  • The message — Maya: nothing came back. Dana: she answered the next morning and still has it.
  • Which version holds? Both can be true at once: Dana was there and Maya did not see her. Nothing in the audio proves anyone lied.

    Respuestas modelo

    • Las tres discrepancias están en la hoja de respuestas del bloque de input. Ninguna prueba que alguien mienta: Dana pudo estar ahí sin que Maya la viera. Esa es la conclusión que se busca.
    • Evidencia modelo: “To me, toxic is behaviour that costs you more than it gives you, every time. There's a difference between a friend who is always late and a friend who goes silent for weeks. That's not toxic, that's just a rough patch — so I'd give Dana the benefit of the doubt, and I'd set boundaries instead of cutting her off.”
    • VOC-04 Dead Words on the Board: escribe las 12 unidades a la vista y borra cada una cuando alguien la use bien hablando. Meta colectiva: pizarrón vacío antes del cierre.

    Errores esperables en B2.1

    • “A person that is toxic” en lugar de “a person who…”. Se corrige en el momento, sin detener la ronda.
    • Usar “that” en una no definitoria entre comas.
    • Describir a una persona real del grupo o de su empresa: se corta y se regresa a Maya y Dana.
    • Confundir “toxic” con “difficult”: es justo lo que la sesión quiere separar.
    • En el Blind Ranking, negociar sin argumento: “ponle 3” no vale; hay que decir por qué.

    Si el grupo va adelantado o atrasado

    Adelantado: Pide una séptima conducta inventada por el grupo y que la coloquen en su orden ya acordado, defendiendo la posición.

    Atrasado: Reduce el ranking de seis conductas a cuatro y da el audio en dos partes, con pausa entre Maya y Dana.

    Dinámicas y límite del nivel

    VOC-04 Dead Words on the Board · GRM-06 Interrogation · LIS-03 Two Versions · SPK-10 Blind Ranking (central). Ninguna repite familia dentro de la sesión.

    No pedir todavía: deducción en pasado (must have), wish / if only, condicionales mixtos, verbos de reporte formales, negociación y conducción de juntas.

    Riesgo corporativo

    Seguro con casos ficticios. Maya y Dana son inventadas. Si alguien empieza a contar un conflicto propio, se agradece y se regresa al caso: esta sesión no pide historias personales.
