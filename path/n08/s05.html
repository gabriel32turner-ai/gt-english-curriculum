Teacher notes · §2 Vocabulary

Pedagógica

  • Paso A (3 min): ▶ cada palabra, el alumno repite en voz alta y dice si ya la conoce. No traduzcas; usa el ejemplo.
  • Paso B (5 min): el pizarrón es colectivo; solo se borra una palabra cuando se usa correctamente HABLANDO (no al leer la tarjeta). El punto es por producir lengua, nunca por rapidez. Si la oración está mal, no se borra: el compañero la mejora y vuelve a intentar.

Crecimiento

  • Estas 11 palabras son el 80 % del correo diario entre proveedor y cliente: “Add them to your next real email this week.”
  • Progreso visible: cuántas ghost words de S01 salen sin ayuda (señal de retención).

Language · What Happened to It?

Station 3 · 12–24 min · Discover 2 · Tool 3 · Practice 2 · GRM-10 3 · Use 2
was / were + participleGRM-10 · OCULTAMIENTO
Discover · 2 min

Read the invoice history. Who is the star of each line?

MON 9:00The invoice was sent to the client.
TUE 10:30Our manager approved it. → It was approved.
WED 8:00Two payments were cancelled.
THU 11:00The new invoice wasn’t received.
FRI 9:30Was the payment approved?
Which words are always the same? Which word changes — was or were? Do we need to say WHO did it?(Respuesta: was/were + participio; was con singular, were con plural; el protagonista es el documento — el agente no importa o no se conoce.)
Tool · 3 min

The past passive for process steps

What it is

The document is the star. We say what happened to it. We don’t say who did it — it is not important, or nobody knows.

The invoice was sent.

How it works

was / were + past participle. One thing → was. Two or more → were. Regular: approved, cancelled, delayed, received, signed. Irregular: sent, paid.

When we use it

For steps of a process and for status: the invoice was sent, received, approved, paid. Today: only these frequent forms, in short sentences.

✓ STATEMENTThe invoice was sent.
✗ NEGATIVEThe invoice wasn’t received.

Teacher notes · §3 Grammar

Pedagógica

  • Tiempos: Discover 2 + Tool 3 = 5 min de explicación tuya (tope). Práctica A (mecánica) 2 min = menos de 1/3 del bloque. GRM-10 3 min (Meaningful). Uso comunicativo 2 min.
  • Discover: NO expliques antes. Muestra las 5 líneas; pregunta “Who is the star?”. Clave: el documento. Contrasta línea 2 (activa → pasiva).
  • Alcance estricto: solo was/were + participio frecuente (sent, approved, cancelled, delayed, received, paid, signed). Si preguntan “by whom?”, responde “we don’t say it today”. Nada de “has been sent” ni “will be sent”.
  • GRM-10: la pregunta cuenta si es VIVA (forma correcta y natural). Modelos aceptables: “When was it sent?” · “What was cancelled? / How many…?” · “Was it received?” · “Why was it delayed?” · “Who approved it?” (activa: aquí sí pedimos el agente) · “When was it paid?”. Cierre declarado: “We built __ of 6 living questions. The trickiest was ___.”
  • Pronunciación: “was” débil /wəz/; el acento cae en el participio.

Engagement

  • El constructor funciona como máquina de tragamonedas: pide a un alumno que elija sujeto y participio “malévolo” y lo digan antes de pulsar.
  • Group: cada quien dice su pregunta en el chat antes de abrir el modelo.

Crecimiento

  • “This one structure lets you talk about any process without blaming anyone. That is why it is the language of professional emails.”
  • Evidencia de progreso: ¿puede formar “Was it…?” solo, sin modelo? Marca 0–6 en GRM-10 y compáralo en S09 (pasiva presente).

Sam: Hello, this is Sam from the accounts team at Norte Supplies. I’m calling about your invoice, number four zero seven two. It was sent on the third of March, but it was not received. The email was delayed, maybe because the file was too big. Your other invoice, number four zero seven three, was received on Monday. It was approved yesterday, and it was paid this morning. The first one has a second problem. The amount on the invoice is one thousand two hundred dollars, but the order was one thousand four hundred dollars. Say it out loud: “Voice __ was the easiest. Voice __ was the hardest because of the ___.”
Teacher notes · §5 Listening (guion y clave)

Pedagógica

  • [PARA GRABAR] Guion literal: 132 palabras, un hablante (Sam, Accounts), tres versiones del MISMO texto. Voz 1 = inglés brasileño (L08-S05-listening-001-sambr) · Voz 2 = inglés de EE. UU. (…002-samus, nativo) · Voz 3 = inglés indio (…003-samin). Velocidad: ritmo natural medio, ≈150 palabras/min.
  • Clave: problema = la factura 4072 no se recibió y el monto está mal · 4072: sent on the 3rd of March, NOT received (email delayed) · 4073: received Monday, approved yesterday, paid this morning · amount: invoice $1,200 vs order $1,400 · due date: the fifteenth · acción: attach a new invoice with the right amount and send it today.
  • Pasivas en el audio: was sent · was not received · was delayed · was received · was approved · was paid. Léxico: invoice, attach, amount, due date, update/send an update, delayed.
  • Ranking: pide DOS cosas: el orden y QUÉ costó (speed / vowels / rhythm / word endings). No hay respuesta única: se valida con evidencia (“I missed ‘due date’ in voice 3”). Dato para el maestro: voz 1, vocales extra al final de palabra y sílabas llenas; voz 2, sílabas débiles y enlaces (“was sent” → /wəzsent/); voz 3, ritmo por sílaba y /t/ /d/ retrofleja.

Engagement

  • No reveles los acentos hasta que ordenen. Después: “¿Con cuál acento tratas tú de verdad en la planta?” Ese es el punto de la dinámica.
  • 1:1: el alumno ordena solo y defiende con una cita del audio; en grupo se vota y se busca consenso.

Crecimiento

  • “Your clients will not speak like the audio of a course. Training your ear with three voices is training for the real call.”
  • Progreso: el alumno nombra el rasgo que le costó. Repite LIS-08 en S11 y S22 y compara qué rasgo ya no le cuesta.

Teacher notes · §6 Speaking

Pedagógica

  • Mecánica fiel a B18-09: mapa con tema central y 4 ramas; el alumno elige una rama, habla, cambia a otra y al final conecta dos ramas. Puntaje: 1 punto por rama visitada con 3 oraciones (usa “+ sentence” para contarlas). Cierre: “We visited __ branches.” Guardrail: el mapa es guía visual, no guion que se lee.

Teacher notes · §7 Mission

Pedagógica

  • Evidencia observable: 3 casos × 4 movimientos = 12; éxito ≥ 10 sin leer. Cada caso exige ≥2 pasivas afirmativas, ≥1 negativa y ≥1 pregunta “Was it…?” hecha al empleado.
  • Casos: 1) factura de proveedor (trabajo): Created Mon · Sent Tue · Received Wed · Approved ✗ (monto equivocado) · Paid ✗. 2) reembolso en línea (vida diaria): Asked · Approved · Sent ✓ · Received ✗ · Receipt attached ✗. 3) formulario escolar: Sent ✓ · Received ✓ · Signed ✗ · Paid ✓ · Confirmed ✗.
  • Giro obligatorio: el empleado dice “Sorry, it was cancelled at the last minute” en un caso (a elección) y el alumno responde con una pregunta y un siguiente paso.
  • Después: éxito → mejora → reparación → 2º intento del caso 1.

Engagement

  • Toma el rol del empleado difícil: respuestas cortas, “I don’t know, the system doesn’t say.” El alumno tiene que reformular.

Crecimiento

  • “This call is real: it is the call you make to a supplier or to a bank. You now have the full script in English.”
  • Guarda el marcador de 12 para comparar en la Misión de S12 (The Information Gap).
