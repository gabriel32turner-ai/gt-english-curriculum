Escuchar a Luis (10 palabras) y decidir A/B/C. 4 preguntas del manual con follow-up, en pasado simple.

2Words⏱ 8 MINtalk ~6

Eight words for a bad day

Teacher note (es)

Palabras del manual: forget, miss, repair, cancel, luckily, suddenly, battery, appointment. Luckily/suddenly son adverbios de reacción y se practican en el speaking. Léxico total: 8.

3Language⏱ 8 MINtalk ~6

First, then, after that, finally

Teacher note (es)

El manual lo define como foco funcional: “seleccionar la frase que hace avanzar la interacción”. Por eso la parte de decisión no es de forma sino de reacción (Oh no! What happened next?). Pasado simple como repaso, sin lección nueva.

4Read & Listen⏱ 16 MINtalk ~7

A day that went wrong

Teacher note (es)

Lectura: texto exacto del manual. Noticing: tocar 2 palabras de orden. 4 tarjetas First/Then/After that/Finally. 1 pregunta de idea general. Escucha: guion del manual (2 líneas) ampliado a 8 líneas. [PARA GRABAR] audio pendiente.

5Speaking⏱ 15 MINtalk ~13

Story Builder: a bad day + a twist

Teacher note (es)

Story Builder: Picture + Twist (15 min). A arma 4 pasos de un mal día; B agrega UN problema inesperado (Suddenly, …). Giro después de la Ronda 1. Al final, una reacción que haga avanzar la interacción.

6Feedback⏱ 6 MINtalk ~5

Second try and feedback

Teacher note (es)

Segundo intento: historia de 4 pasos en 60–90 s sin tarjetas. Feedback: 2 aciertos + hasta 3 mejoras + Next-use phrase. Transferencia: nota de voz de 2–4 min.

TTeacher key

Key and checks

Answers are flexible because production is personal

Reading check: Luis forgot his keys, returned for them, missed his bus, called his supervisor, his computer did not start, IT repaired it; he finished his task. Listening check: Luis forgot his keys, missed the bus, called his supervisor, his computer did not start, IT repaired it, he finished his task. Useful output: Sequencers (first, then, after that, finally) with past simple, plus reaction phrases that move the interaction forward. Accept intelligible variation; repair only errors that block time, politeness or the next step.
