How the level is measured

Evidence you can hear, at fixed points

Each block of 10 sessions mixes development, integration and evidence. The checkpoints sit in the same place in the three Básico levels.

Evaluation rhythm

  1. S01BaselineThe final speaking task, with no preparation and the same rubric as Session 40.
  2. S10Quiz 1 · covers Sessions 1–9Short comprehension, controlled use and a micro speaking evidence.
  3. S20Quiz 2 · covers Sessions 11–19Recovery of earlier language and a short performance in context.
  4. S30Quiz 3 · covers Sessions 21–29Application of the block focuses and selective correction.
  5. S40Quiz 4 · covers Sessions 31–39 and the closeClosing quiz + individual performance compared with the Session 1 baseline.

What each quiz covers. The quiz in Sessions 10, 20 and 30 covers the nine sessions before it. The focus presented that same day is practiced, and assessed at the next control point.

Session types

    How much support you get

    The written support goes down as you advance. Sessions of this level at each step:

      What counts as evidence

      • 1Clarity. The main message is understood, even with isolated errors.
      • 2Interaction. You answer, ask or repair, according to the block focus.
      • 3Target language. The structure appears inside a task, not only in exercises.
      • 4Autonomy. Less model, script and visible vocabulary each time.
      • 5Transfer. The task changes partner, place, detail or option without changing the language level.

      The language of this level

      Main language: full present simple · do/does · frequency · present continuous · can/can’t · like/love/hate + -ing · be going to in affirmative, negative and question · comparatives with -er and more · because as a minimal connector · repair chunks.

      Kept for later levels: the past, present perfect, will as a system, have to, should, conditionals, superlatives, complex negotiation and debate. Best appears only as a fixed word (the best option).
