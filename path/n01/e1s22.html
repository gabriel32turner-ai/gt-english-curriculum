Grammar · from… to… · until

14–25 min · Discover → understand → practice
Opening hoursGRM-15 Tic-Tac-Toe
The tool

The hours bar · move the start and the end

from 9 to 4 = de… a… · until midnight = hasta · it opens / closes at…
+The bank opens at 9. It's open from 9 to 4. The pharmacy is open until midnight.
−It isn't open on Sunday.
?Is it open on Sunday? What time is it open until?
closed = 1 syllableOpen

from… to… = de… a… (from 9 to 4) · until = hasta (until midnight). No se mezclan: from 9 until to 4.

opens / closes llevan -s porque el sujeto es it (el lugar): The bank opens at 9.

open también es adjetivo: It's open. / It's closed.

Monday to Friday = de lunes a viernes · 24/7 = todo el día, todos los días.

Mission · Sunday Errands

Real-life taskEvidence
Your mission

Sunday. 4 errands. Only 2 phone calls.

You need: groceries, clean clothes, dog food and medicine. You don't know the Sunday hours. You can call only 2 places. Listen, write the hours, and decide your plan in 3 sentences: “First, I go to… because it's open until…”.

Group: pairs. A calls (the teacher or B reads the recording), B writes. Then change.

1:1: the teacher answers the phone as each place (or plays the recording).
