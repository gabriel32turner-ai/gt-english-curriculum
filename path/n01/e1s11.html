Grammar · This one? Those?

14–25 min · Discover → understand → practice
this / that / these / thoseGRM-02 Human Sentence
Discover

At the lost-and-found desk. Find the 4 pointing words.

Hi. Is this your jacket?

No, it isn't. That jacket is mine — the blue one.

And these sunglasses?

Yes! These are my sunglasses. And those keys?

Sorry, those aren't yours.

Which words are for one thing? For many?(Respuesta: this/that = uno · these/those = varios)
The tool

Near or far? One or many?

near: this · these  |  far: that · those

?Is this your umbrella? Are those your keys?
thisthesethatthosetongue between the teeth — not “dis”

Cerca: this (este/esta) para una cosa, these (estos/estas) para varias.

Lejos: that (ese/aquel) para una cosa, those (esos/aquellos) para varias.

Con be: this/that + is, these/those + are: These are my keys. Pregunta: Is this…? Are those…?

Colores y adjetivos van antes de la cosa y no cambian en plural: a red cap, two red caps (no “reds caps”). Mine = mío/mía: It's mine.

Teacher notes · §3 Grammar

Pedagógica

  • Explicación ≤5 min: diálogo → herramienta cerca/lejos × uno/varios (señala con la mano en cámara) → tabla → /ð/.
  • GRM-02 escalada (S01 tenía 5 chips): ahora 5–7 chips con demostrativo + color. Récord de tiempo del grupo por oración. Luego meaningful: cada alumno toma un objeto real y dice “This is my…”.

Engagement

  • Señalar físicamente: pide que toquen algo cerca (this) y señalen algo lejos (that) en su cuarto.
  • Cierre: “Sentence 5 in __ seconds. New group record.”

Crecimiento

  • Demostrativos vuelven en S26 (How much is this?) y S29 (This is my friend).
