1Warm-up⏱ 5 MINstudent talk ~4 min

Who is in your circle?

Teacher notes · Notas del maestro

Recycling S05 (he / she, his / her, family, friend): open with “Who is she?” looking at a photo. Then they hear Paola (14 words: “I have a brother…”) and choose WHAT they say about their own circle: a brother, a sister or a friend. All three options are right: the choice is personal. Accept “I have a sister” even with errors in the name: only recasts. 1:1: you also say one sentence about your own family. Group: in pairs.Reciclaje de S05 (he / she, his / her, family, friend): abre con “Who is she?” mirando una foto. Luego escuchan a Paola (14 palabras: “I have a brother…”) y eligen QUÉ dicen de su propio círculo: un hermano, una hermana o un amigo. Las tres opciones son correctas: la decisión es personal. Acepta “I have a sister” aunque haya errores en el nombre: solo recasts. 1:1: tú también dices una oración sobre tu familia. Grupo: en parejas.

2Words⏱ 11 MINstudent talk ~7 min

Eight words for your circle

Teacher notes · Notas del maestro

Words from the program sheet: brother, sister, colleague, computer, idea, small business, plus mother and father (kinship opens today; S05 left it closed). Each card: you say it, students repeat twice and say THEIR sentence with a real person. Pronunciation: brother / mother / father (the th is voiced; do not say “brozer” or “fader”), colleague (KOL-eeg), idea (eye-DEE-a). Match photos, listen and choose, sort man/woman and three oral sentences. “Have” and “has” are not cards: they are the grammar. No clue game today.Léxico de la ficha: brother, sister, colleague, computer, idea, small business, más mother y father (hoy se abren los parentescos; S05 los dejó cerrados). Cada tarjeta: tú la dices, los alumnos repiten dos veces y dicen SU oración con una persona real. Pronunciación: brother / mother / father (la th es sonora; no digas “broder” ni “fader”), colleague (KOL-ig), idea (ai-DI-a). Emparejar fotos, escuchar y elegir, clasificar hombre/mujer y tres oraciones orales. “Have” y “has” no son tarjetas: son la gramática. Hoy no hay juego de pistas.

6Speaking⏱ 11 MINstudent talk ~9 min

My circle, two rounds

Teacher notes · Notas del maestro

Model 1 min · Build 1 (pick) · Round 1 (about 1.5 min) · Build 2 (information gap) + change + Round 2 (about 1.5 min) · question ladder (6 min). Build 1: choose who is in your circle, what that person has and your question; say four sentences with have / has. Build 2: A and B know three people each and what they have; they ask with Who / Is it… and say it all with has. Questions with have (Do you have…?) are NOT used. The change comes AFTER Round 1: new partner, new person, no cards or one more fact. Never accept a one-word answer.Modelo 1 min · Build 1 (pick) · Ronda 1 (cerca de 1.5 min) · Build 2 (information gap) + cambio + Ronda 2 (cerca de 1.5 min) · escalera (6 min). Build 1: eligen quién está en su círculo, qué tiene esa persona y su pregunta; dicen cuatro oraciones con have / has. Build 2: A y B conocen a tres personas cada uno y lo que tienen; preguntan con Who / Is it… y lo dicen todo con has. NO se usan preguntas con have (Do you have…?). El cambio llega DESPUÉS de la Ronda 1: nueva pareja, nueva persona, sin tarjetas o un dato más. Nunca aceptes una palabra.

Minute by minute · Minuto a minuto

0–1′Model: listen to Emma.Modelo: escuchan a Emma.
1–4′Build 1 (pick) and Round 1.Build 1 (pick) y Ronda 1.
4–6′Build 2 (gap), change and Round 2.Build 2 (gap), cambio y Ronda 2.
6–11′Question ladder in pairs.Escalera de preguntas en parejas.

Plan B: If time is short, skip Build 2 and go straight to the ladder with four questions.Si falta tiempo, salta el Build 2 y ve directo a la escalera con cuatro preguntas.

Common errors · Errores comunes

  • “He have” / “I has”: the verb does not match the subject.“He have” / “I has”: el verbo no concuerda con el sujeto.
  • “Haves” as a form of have.“Haves” como forma de have.
  • Omitting the article: “She has sister”.Omitir el artículo: “She has sister”.
  • Using “I am” to mean “I have”: “I am a brother”.Usar “I am” para decir “I have”: “I am a brother”.
