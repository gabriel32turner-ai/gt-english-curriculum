Teacher notes · §1 Warm-up

Pedagógica

  • Reciclaje S25 (60 s): “How many nights? — Two nights, please.” Pregunta rápida: “How many coffees per day?”

Grammar · How much is / are…?

14–25 min · Discover → understand → practice
It's… / They're…GRM-16 Mystery Box
The tool

The price tag machine · one or more?

ONE → How much IS … ? IT'S …  |  MORE → How much ARE … ? THEY'RE …
?How much is this? How much are these?
+It's forty pesos. They're two dollars each.
−It isn't cheap. It's expensive.
150 = one hundred and fiftydollarsFIFty / fifTEEN

How much = ¿cuánto cuesta? Para precios. (En S25 aprendiste How many = ¿cuántos? para contar.)

Uno: How much is this coffee? → It's forty pesos.   Varios: How much are these cookies? → They're twenty pesos each.

Números: 150 = one hundred and fifty. $3.50 = “three fifty”. $7.50 = “seven fifty”.

Ojo: no digas “How much cost?”; di How much is it?

Teacher notes · §3 Grammar

Pedagógica

  • Explicación ≤5 min con la máquina de etiquetas: elige producto, uno o varios, precio → la pregunta y respuesta cambian (is/It's vs. are/They're) y el número aparece en palabras.
  • GRM-16 (Mystery Box): cada caja tiene producto real con precio; los equipos apuestan; un alumno pregunta “How much is it?” y otro lee el precio con “It's…/They're…”. La apuesta más cercana gana 2; exacta, 3.

Engagement

  • Cierre: “The surprise price was ___.”

Crecimiento

  • “How much” solo para precios (incontables como money/water se ven en otro nivel).

Teacher notes · §6 Speaking

Pedagógica

  • B18-06 Budget Challenge: ronda 1 con precios normales; ronda 2 todo sube 20% (los precios se recalculan solos) y el presupuesto sigue en $500. El comprador NO ve los precios en la ronda 2 si el maestro activa “🙈 Hide prices”: tiene que preguntar con 🔊 Ask para ver cada precio.
  • Meta: comprar en los 3 puestos sin pasar de $500, usando How much is/are…?, It's/They're…, That's cheap/expensive, Here you are.

Engagement

  • Cierre: “I have ___ pesos left. The expensive thing was ___.”

Crecimiento

  • Errores para §8: “How much cost”, “How much is these”, “one hundred fifty”.
