3Language⏱ 11 MINstudent talk ~7 min

Spell it · say the numbers · repair it

Teacher notes · Notas del maestro

Two short boards first (alphabet and numbers, listen and say), then three idea screens: How do you spell that? / Is that right? · What’s your + data / My + data + is · Can you repeat that, please? Spanish speakers mix the vowels: E = “ee”, I = “eye”, A = “ay”; and say “arroba” and “punto”: in English @ is “at” and . is “dot”. Numbers are said one by one; “double five” = 55. Be questions only; the question with do is a fixed block. Practice: choose, fix, true/false, match and say.Primero dos tableros cortos (alfabeto y números, escuchar y decir), luego tres pantallas de idea: How do you spell that? / Is that right? · What’s your + dato / My + dato + is · Can you repeat that, please? El hispanohablante confunde las vocales: E = “ii”, I = “ai”, A = “ei”; y dice “arroba” y “punto”: en inglés @ es “at” y . es “dot”. Los números se dicen uno por uno; “double five” = 55. Solo preguntas con be; la pregunta con do es un bloque fijo. Práctica: elegir, reparar, V/F, emparejar y decir.

Minute by minute · Minuto a minuto

0–3′Alphabet board, numbers board, three idea cards.Tablero del alfabeto, tablero de números, tres tarjetas de idea.
3–8′Choose (4), fix (4), true/false (4), match (6).Elegir (4), reparar (4), V/F (4), emparejar (6).
8–11′Say it: numbers, an email and a phone check.Decirlo: números, un correo y una confirmación de teléfono.

Plan B: Skip the pairs card and the match; keep the boards, choose, fix and say.Salta la tarjeta de pares y el emparejar; conserva los tableros, elegir, reparar y decir.

6Speaking⏱ 11 MINstudent talk ~9 min

Contact card, two rounds

Teacher notes · Notas del maestro

Model 1 min · Build 1 (interview) · Round 1 (about 1.5 min) · Build 2 (information gap: the contact card) + change + Round 2 (about 1.5 min) · question ladder (6 min). Build 1: Host answers with the data on a photo card; Guest asks, asks for the spelling and checks (“Is that right?”). Build 2: A and B have different cards; they complete the contact card and check it. The change comes AFTER Round 1: new partner, new card, close the card or a noisy line (Can you repeat that?). Never accept a one-word answer. Cards are fictional data: do not ask for students’ real phone numbers. 1:1: you are the partner. Group: pairs in rooms.Modelo 1 min · Build 1 (interview) · Ronda 1 (cerca de 1.5 min) · Build 2 (information gap: la tarjeta de contacto) + cambio + Ronda 2 (cerca de 1.5 min) · escalera (6 min).
