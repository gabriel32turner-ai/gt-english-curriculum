Presente simple = cosas que haces normalmente, todos los días: I get up at 6.

Con I, you, we, they el verbo no cambia: I work, we work, they work. (Con he/she cambia: lo vemos en la sesión 21.)

Para preguntar: hoy usa And you? o What about you? — la pregunta completa “What time do you…?” la dice el maestro; tú la entiendes.

Ojo: go home (sin “to”) · have breakfast / lunch / dinner (no “take”).

Mission · Roommate Interview

47–55 min · 6 actions with times · find 2 conflicts
Real-life taskEvidence
Your mission

New apartment. One bathroom. One kitchen.

You and your new roommate tell your days: 6 actions with times. Find 2 conflicts — the same time in the bathroom or in the kitchen! Then say the problem: “I get up at 6 and you get up at 6. One bathroom!”

Group: pairs. Each one says his / her day; the other writes it.

1:1: the teacher is the roommate (card “Leo”).

Teacher notes · §7 Mission

Pedagógica

  • Tabla “Me / My roommate”: el alumno llena su hora y la del compañero (lo que escucha). “Find the conflicts” marca horas iguales en baño (get up, go to bed) o cocina (breakfast, lunch, dinner). Éxito: 6 acciones con hora + 2 conflictos detectados y dichos. Evidencia: tabla completa.
  • Tarjeta “Leo” para 1:1 o para quien no quiera usar su rutina real.

Engagement

  • Bonus: propongan solución con lo que saben: “I get up at 6. You get up at 6:30. OK?”

Crecimiento
