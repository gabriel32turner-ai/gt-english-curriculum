1Warm-up⏱ 5 MINstudent talk ~4 min

Who is in your photo?

Teacher notes · Notas del maestro

Recycling S01–S04 (be, roles, spelling, contact): open with the first two ladder questions (role and last name spelled). Then they hear Paola (12 words) and each student chooses ONE real photo on their phone: family, friends or team. All three options are valid: the choice is personal and makes them say “This is my…” with real data. 1:1: you also show a photo of yours. Group: 20 seconds per person with the photo to the camera. If someone does not want to show personal photos, use the photo on the page. No form correction here, only recasts (“Oh, she’s your friend!”).Reciclaje de S01–S04 (be, roles, deletreo, contacto): abre con las dos primeras preguntas de la escalera (rol y apellido deletreado). Luego escuchan a Paola (12 palabras) y cada alumno elige UNA foto real de su teléfono: familia, amigos o equipo. Las tres opciones son válidas: la decisión es personal y obliga a decir “This is my…” con un dato real. 1:1: tú también muestras una foto tuya. Grupo: 20 segundos por persona con la foto a la cámara. Si alguien no quiere mostrar fotos personales, usa la foto de la página. Sin corrección de forma aquí, solo recasts (“Oh, she’s your friend!”).

2Words⏱ 11 MINstudent talk ~7 min

Eight words for your people

Teacher notes · Notas del maestro

Words from the program sheet: family, coworker, friend, manager, team, his, her, plus neighbor. Each card: you say it, students repeat twice and say THEIR sentence with a real person. his / her appear as words here and are worked on in Language. Pronunciation: “neighbor” is not read as written (NEY-bor); “coworker” is stressed on “co”. Do not open kinship (mother, brother, sister): that is S06. Match photos, listen and choose, sort “his or her” and three oral sentences. No clue game today.Léxico de la ficha: family, coworker, friend, manager, team, his, her, más neighbor. Cada tarjeta: tú la dices, los alumnos repiten dos veces y dicen SU oración con una persona real. his / her entran aquí como palabras y se trabajan en Language. Pronunciación: “neighbor” no se lee como se escribe (NEI-bor); “coworker” lleva el acento en “co”. No abras parentescos (mother, brother, sister): llegan en S06. Emparejar fotos, escuchar y elegir, clasificar “his o her” y tres oraciones orales. Hoy no hay juego de pistas.

3Language⏱ 11 MINstudent talk ~7 min

He’s · She’s · They’re · his · her

Teacher notes · Notas del maestro

Explanation: max. 3 min, three screens: He’s / She’s / They’re · my / your / his / her · Who is he? / Who is she? Priority errors for Spanish speakers: 1) omitting the subject (“Is my coworker”); 2) choosing his / her by the thing and not by the owner (“She’s my friend. His name is Ana”); 3) confusing He’s with His (“He’s name is Leo”); 4) “They is”. Trick: point at the person in the photo: man = his, woman = her. To say no: “No.” + the right sentence; isn’t / aren’t arrive in S33. Do not use have / has: they arrive in S06.Explicación: máx. 3 min, tres pantallas: He’s / She’s / They’re · my / your / his / her · Who is he? / Who is she? Errores prioritarios del hispanohablante: 1) omitir el sujeto (“Is my coworker”); 2) elegir his / her por la cosa y no por el dueño (“She’s my friend. His name is Ana”); 3) confundir He’s con His (“He’s name is Leo”); 4) “They is”. Truco: señala a la persona de la foto: hombre = his, mujer = her. Para decir que no: “No.” + la oración correcta; isn’t / aren’t llegan en S33. No uses have / has: llegan en S06.

Minute by minute · Minuto a minuto

0–3′Three idea cards.Tres tarjetas de idea.
3–8′Choose (4), fix (4), true/false (4), match (6).Elegir (4), reparar (4), V/F (4), emparejar (6).
8–11′Look and say: three photos, two sentences each.Mirar y decir: tres fotos, dos oraciones cada una.

Plan B: Skip the pairs card and the match; keep choose, fix, true/false and say.Salta la tarjeta de pares y el emparejar; conserva elegir, reparar, V/F y decir.

6Speaking⏱ 11 MINstudent talk ~9 min

My people map, two rounds

Teacher notes · Notas del maestro

Model 1 min · Build 1 (information gap) · Round 1 (about 1.5 min) · Build 2 (interview) + change + Round 2 (about 1.5 min) · question ladder (6 min). Build 1: A and B see the same four photos described by their looks; each knows the facts of three people and asks the other three (“Who is he? What’s his name?”). Build 2: Host says 3 sentences per photo without reading; Guest asks at least 4 questions. The change comes AFTER Round 1: new partner, real people from the phone, no cards or one more fact. Ladder: 8 questions about their real circle; the speaker chooses he / she and his / her by the person. Never accept a one-word answer.Modelo 1 min · Build 1 (information gap) · Ronda 1 (cerca de 1.5 min) · Build 2 (interview) + cambio + Ronda 2 (cerca de 1.5 min) · escalera (6 min). Build 1: A y B ven las mismas fotos descritas por su aspecto; cada uno conoce los datos de tres personas y pregunta por las otras tres (“Who is he? What’s his name?”). Build 2: el Host dice 3 oraciones por foto sin leer; el Guest hace mínimo 4 preguntas. El cambio llega DESPUÉS de la Ronda 1: nueva pareja, personas reales del teléfono, sin tarjetas o un dato más. Escalera: 8 preguntas sobre su círculo real; quien habla elige he / she y his / her según la persona. Nunca aceptes una palabra.

Minute by minute · Minuto a minuto

0–1′Model: listen to the narrator.Modelo: escuchan al narrador.
1–4′Build 1 (information gap) and Round 1.Build 1 (information gap) y Ronda 1.
4–6′Build 2 (interview), change and Round 2.Build 2 (interview), cambio y Ronda 2.
6–11′Question ladder in pairs.Escalera de preguntas en parejas.

Plan B: If time is short, skip Build 2 and go straight to the ladder with four questions.Si falta tiempo, salta el Build 2 y ve directo a la escalera con cuatro preguntas.

7Feedback⏱ 4 MINstudent talk ~3 min

Second try and feedback

Teacher notes · Notas del maestro

Individual evidence 45–60 s with no cards: three real people (a photo on the phone or a people map drawn on a sheet), who each one is, their role or city and at least one sentence with his or her. The page does not record: the student records on their phone and sends the voice note. Feedback: 2 successes + up to 3 upgrades + 1 phrase for this week. Check above all that there is a subject (He’s / She’s / They’re) and that his / her matches the owner.Evidencia individual 45–60 s sin tarjetas: tres personas reales (una foto del teléfono o un mapa de personas dibujado en una hoja), quién es cada una, su rol o ciudad y al menos una oración con his o her. La página no graba: el alumno graba con su teléfono y envía la nota de voz. Feedback: 2 aciertos + hasta 3 mejoras + 1 frase para esta semana. Revisa sobre todo que haya sujeto (He’s / She’s / They’re) y que his / her corresponda al dueño.
