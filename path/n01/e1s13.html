there'sthere areKI-tchen

¿Qué es? There is / There are = hay. Sirve para decir qué existe en un lugar.

Una cosa: There's a kitchen (there's = there is). Varias: There are two bedrooms.

Negativo: There isn't a garden. There aren't windows.

Ojo: para describir un lugar no se dice “Have a kitchen” ni “Is a kitchen”: siempre There's a kitchen. La pregunta Is there…? se practica en la sesión 15.

Your mission

You are the host. Sell your place!

Choose a place (a card — or your real home). Describe it in 6 sentences with there's / there are. The guest listens, asks “Sorry? Can you repeat that?” if necessary, and decides: “Yes! I book it because…” or “No, thanks. There isn't…”.

Group: pairs. A = host, B = guest. Then change places.
