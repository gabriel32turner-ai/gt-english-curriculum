Grammar · didn't & Did you…?

13–25 min · Discover → understand → practice
New pointGRM-18 Three Lives
Discover

Read the notes. What's different from "I cooked…"?

📝🧊

I didn't touch your yogurt.

Did you taste the soup too?

I didn't cook on Sunday.

What comes right after didn't and Did?(Respuesta: el verbo base, sin -ed)
The tool

didn't / Did + base verb

1 · Negative

didn't + base

I didn't touch it. He didn't cook on Sunday. (never "didn't cooked")

2 · Question

Did + subject + base

Did you open my fridge? Did she finish the pasta?

3 · Short answer

Yes, I did. / No, I didn't.

No full sentence needed in fast conversation.

FormExampleWatch out
+ (S6)I cooked rice yesterday.past form of the verb
− didn'tI didn't touch it.base verb, NOT "didn't touched"
? DidDid you open the fridge?base verb, NOT "Did you opened"

Con was/were no se usa did (eso es otro tema). Hoy solo verbos regulares y los verbos base ya conocidos (eat, have) dentro de la pregunta — el pasado irregular producido (ate, had) llega en Sesión 9.

¿Qué es? El negativo del pasado simple se forma con didn't + verbo base: I didn't cook. La pregunta se forma con Did + sujeto + verbo base: Did you cook?

¿Cómo se usa? El verbo NUNCA lleva -ed después de didn't o Did — ese es el error más común ("didn't cooked", "Did you opened").

¿Cómo se responde? Respuesta corta: Yes, I did. / No, I didn't. No se repite el verbo.

¿Para qué hoy? Para investigar un caso: preguntar qué pasó y qué NO pasó con el refrigerador compartido.

Teacher notes · §3 Grammar

Pedagógica

  • Punto NUEVO del día. Deja que el grupo encuentre el patrón en Discover antes de nombrar la regla.

Teacher notes · §7 Mission

Pedagógica
