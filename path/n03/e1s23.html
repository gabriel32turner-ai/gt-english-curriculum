Grammar · a lot of / a few / a little

13–25 min · Discover → understand → bet
New pointGRM-01 Bet the Sentence · apuesta
Discover

Read the chat. How many quantity words do you see?

🍳🥚

There's a lot of rice in this pot!

And a few eggs on top.

Do we need a little salt too?

Just a little — we don't have a lot of time!

Count them. How many do you see?(Respuesta: 5 · a lot of, a few, a little, a little, a lot of)
The tool

a lot of / a few / a little + noun

1 · A LOT OF

a lot of + noun

mucho / muchos — countable AND uncountable. a lot of rice · a lot of eggs

2 · A FEW

a few + plural noun

unos pocos — COUNTABLE only. a few eggs, a few tomatoes (never "a few salt")

3 · A LITTLE

a little + noun

un poco — UNCOUNTABLE only. a little salt, a little sugar (never "a little eggs")

Example
+There's a lot of rice. We have a few eggs. I need a little salt.
−There isn't a lot of milk. We don't have a lot of time.
?Do you have a lot of…? Do we need a few…? Do you add a little…?

A lot of = mucho/muchos (contables e incontables). A few = unos pocos (SOLO contables: a few eggs). A little = un poco (SOLO incontables: a little salt). No confundas: a few eggs / a little milk.

¿Qué es? Son tres formas de decir CUÁNTO hay de algo, sin dar un número exacto.

¿Cómo se usa? A lot of funciona con sustantivos contables Y incontables (a lot of rice / a lot of eggs). A few SOLO con contables, en plural (a few eggs). A little SOLO con incontables (a little salt). Nunca mezcles: "a few salt" y "a little eggs" son errores.

¿Cómo suena? "A lot of" se enlaza /ə ˈlɑt əv/. "A few" /ə fjuː/. "A little" /ə ˈlɪɾl/ — la "t" suena suave, casi como una "d" rápida.

¿Para qué hoy? Para explicar una receta familiar sin números exactos — exactamente como la usa tu Mission de hoy.

Watch out

Mistakes at this level

I need a few sugar.I need a little sugar.sugar is uncountable → a little, never a few.
She has a little eggs.She has a few eggs.eggs is countable → a few, never a little.
He has a few time today.He has a little time today.time is uncountable → a little, never a few.
Teacher notes · §3 Grammar

Pedagógica

  • Punto NUEVO del día. Da tiempo real al Discover antes de nombrar la regla. Practice A es mecánica (máx. 1/3 del bloque).
  • Bet the Sentence es APUESTA: el alumno apuesta 1-3 puntos antes de decidir si la oración es correcta o tiene error; "corregir" mal una que ya estaba bien también le cuesta puntos. Regla 3-3-3.
  • Nivel: SOLO estos tres cuantificadores. Sin too/enough/fewer/less, sin comparaciones de cantidad.

Engagement

  • "Bet smart — can you spot the real mistakes without losing points on sentences that are already fine?"

MY GRANDMOTHER'S RICE

My grandmother's rice is famous in our family. She never follows a recipe with exact numbers — she just knows. First, she puts a little oil in the pan. Then she adds a lot of rice and cooks it slowly. She always adds a few tomatoes for color and a little salt for taste. "You don't need a lot of salt," she says, "just a little is enough." At the end, she adds a few eggs on top. My grandmother never measures anything, but her rice always tastes perfect, and everybody asks for more!

Read & answer

What does the recipe say?

[PARA GRABAR]
CHEF:Let's make rice with eggs. It's easy, and you only need a few things. First, put a little oil in the pan. Then add a lot of rice and cook it for ten minutes. After that, add a few tomatoes and a little salt. Finally, put some eggs on top. Cook them for three minutes. You don't have to use a lot of time, and you don't have to buy a lot of food. Enjoy!
Pronunciation

a lot of / a few / a little — linked and reduced

Teacher notes · §5 Listening

Pedagógica

  • Guion de 1 hablante, para grabar: CHEF (mujer, British English, lenta) — personaje NUEVO del nivel.
  • Answer key — Global: (a) rice with eggs. Order It: 1) a little oil in the pan → 2) a lot of rice, cook 10 min → 3) a few tomatoes and a little salt → 4) eggs on top, cook 3 min.
  • Order It (RANKING): Regla de cierre — el alumno dice el orden completo en voz alta al terminar, no solo hace clic.

Engagement

  • "Listen once, then put the steps in the right order — say it out loud when you're done."

Crecimiento

  • El orden de estos 4 pasos es la base de la receta de 7 pasos de la Mission.
