Discover

+ Affirmative

went / saw / bought

We went to Oaxaca. She took a photo.

− Negative

didn't + go

We didn't take a taxi. I didn't eat there. (verbo vuelve a la forma base)

? Question

Did you go?

Did you go by bus? What did you buy? (verbo vuelve a la forma base)

Pronunciation

Sound groups

¿Qué es? Algunos verbos no llevan -ed en pasado: go → went, see → saw. Son irregulares — hay que aprender su forma de memoria.

¿Cómo se usa? Grupo 1 de hoy: went, had, saw, ate, bought, took, got, came. Solo el afirmativo cambia de forma.

¿Cómo suena? Se agrupan por sonido: saw–bought /ɔː/ · took /ʊ/ · came–ate /eɪ/ · went–had–got /e æ ɒ/. Cuidado especial con ate /eɪt/ y bought /bɔːt/.

¿Para qué hoy? En negativo y en pregunta el verbo regresa a su forma base: I didn't go, Did you go? Eso NO cambia — solo el afirmativo usa went/saw/bought.

Teacher notes · §6 Speaking

Pedagógica

  • Reglas en 3 frases: "Start the story. I'll add a twist. Then retell it without your notes."

Mission · Trip Report

47–55 min · Tell it · Ask · Retell without the card
Character cardEvidence
Your mission

Pick a destination card. Tell your partner the trip in 6 sentences using 6 different irregular verbs. Your partner asks 2 "Did you…?" questions. Then tell it again WITHOUT looking at the card.

Success: 6 different irregulars + 2 negatives with didn't + 2 correct partner questions.

Group: each card goes to one student in a breakout room. Partners swap after 4 minutes.

1:1: the teacher plays the partner and asks the 2 questions.
