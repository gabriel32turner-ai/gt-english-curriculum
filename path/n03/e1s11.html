Grammar · there was / there were

13–25 min · Discover → understand → practice
New pointGRM-17 Build-Up Sentence
Discover

Read the sentences. One is about ONE thing, one is about TWO things. What changes?

🎞️🥐

There was a cinema on this corner.

There were two bakeries next to it.

There wasn't a supermarket.

Compare: was vs. were — what's different about the noun?(Respuesta: was = una cosa, were = dos o más)
The tool

There was / were — singular and plural

+ Affirmative

There was / were

There was a market. There were two bakeries.

− Negative

wasn't / weren't

There wasn't a supermarket. There weren't many cars.

? Question

Was/Were there…?

Was there a park? Were there trees?

Affirmative (+)Negative (−)Question (?)
SingularThere was a cinema.There wasn't a supermarket.Was there a park? — Yes, there was.
PluralThere were two bakeries.There weren't many cars.Were there trees? — No, there weren't.
Pronunciation

Linked and reduced

Marcadores de tiempo: a long time ago, then, before. Solo lugares y objetos contables sencillos hoy — some/any llega en la Sesión 21.

¿Qué es? There was/were describe qué había en un lugar en el pasado. There was = había (una cosa); there were = había (dos o más cosas).

¿Cómo se usa? Negativo: there wasn't (singular) / there weren't (plural). Pregunta: Was there…? / Were there…? con respuesta corta: Yes, there was. / No, there weren't.

¿Cómo suena? "There was" y "there were" se enlazan como una sola palabra: /ðer wəz/, /ðer wɜr/. La "th" es sonora (lengua entre los dientes, con vibración). "Weren't" suena /wɜrnt/, nunca "ueren-t" separado.

¿Para qué hoy? Para describir cómo era tu esquina o tu calle antes, y compararla con cómo es ahora — eso es exactamente la Misión.

Teacher notes · §3 Grammar

Pedagógica

My street then and now

When I was a child, there was a small cinema on our corner, and there were two bakeries next to it. There wasn't a supermarket, but there was a market on Sundays. There weren't many cars, so the street was quiet. Now there's a big supermarket, and there are three bakeries. The cinema isn't there anymore, and there's a lot of traffic. I miss the old cinema, but I love the new bakery. My street is different now, but it's still home.

Read & answer

Then or now?
