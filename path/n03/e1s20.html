Count them. How many rule-words? How many past-words?(Respuesta: 2 de regla · Do, have to — 1 de pasado · Was)
The tool

Two systems you already know — now together

1 · The rule (today's notices)

have to / don't have to

You have to show your sticker. You don't have to pay for the laundry room.

2 · Asking about a rule

Do I have to…?

Do I have to book it? — Does she have to sign in?

3 · Asking about the past

Was / Were there…?

Was there a notice yesterday? Were there two notices last week?

Statement / QuestionUse
RuleYou have to take out the trash before seven.obligation
No ruleYou don't have to pay for the pool.not necessary
Ask a ruleDo I have to book the room? / Does he have to sign in?checking a rule
Ask the pastWas there a notice yesterday? Were there two notices?checking what happened

Nothing new today: you're putting Session 17–19's rules next to Session 5–15's past, on the same notice board.

¿Qué es? No es gramática nueva: es juntar have to / don't have to / Do…have to (reglas de S17–S19) con was/were (pasado de S5–S15), ambas en el contexto de los avisos de un edificio.

¿Cómo se usa? Para una regla de HOY usamos have to / don't have to / Do…have to. Para preguntar qué pasó ANTES (si había un aviso ayer, la semana pasada) usamos was/were.

¿Para qué hoy? Para resolver el rompecabezas del tablón: necesitas explicar reglas actuales Y confirmar avisos pasados.

Cuidado: after have to / don't have to the verb is always base form: have to pay, never have to pays or have to paid.

Practice A · Mechanical

Choose: have to · don't have to · Do · Was

Practice B · GRM-20

Secret Card — find the rule that works for YOUR situation

Tap your card to see your secret situation. Then answer the question with the rule that is true for YOU — not for everyone. Keep asking until the group agrees on a rule that works for every situation on the board.

Watch out

Mistakes at this level

You have to payed the fee.You have to pay the fee.After have to, the verb is always base form.
She don't have to book it.She doesn't have to book it.he/she/it → doesn't have to, not don't.
Is there a notice yesterday?Was there a notice yesterday?Yesterday = past → was/were, not is/are.
Teacher notes · §3 Grammar

Pedagógica

  • 100% integración — cero explicación nueva. Máximo 5 minutos de foco explícito; el resto es producción.
  • Practice A es mecánica (máx. 1/3 del bloque). Secret Card es comunicativa: cada alumno tiene una situación distinta (≤15 palabras) y solo puede responder según SU tarjeta — obliga a escuchar antes de aplicar la regla general.
  • Grupo: cada alumno voltea su propia tarjeta; el grupo negocia en voz alta hasta encontrar una regla general que no contradiga ninguna tarjeta. 1:1: tú y el alumno se turnan las 4 tarjetas.
  • Level boundary — no te salgas de esto: sin must/can't como prohibición; "isn't allowed" solo aparece en un aviso de §4/§5 como recepción, nunca en producción del alumno.

Engagement

  • "Four different people, one rule that works for all of them — that's today's real challenge."

Crecimiento

  • have to / don't have to / Do…have to llega a su checkpoint en la Sesión 24.

Teacher notes · §4 Reading

Pedagógica

  • Primera lectura en silencio (1 min) buscando SOLO horas, días y cifras. Segunda lectura en voz alta: un alumno lee cada aviso.

Teacher notes · §5 Listening

Pedagógica

  • Antes de dar play: el grupo lee el tablón real (3 datos). Después escucha al manager y marca True/Lie comparando contra ese tablón — no contra su memoria.
  • Guion de 76 palabras, 1 hablante. Para grabar: MANAGER = hombre, inglés canadiense, velocidad media-lenta, 0.83. Personaje NUEVO del nivel.
  • Answer key: pool until 10 (tablón dice 8 p.m.) · trash Tuesday & Thursday (tablón dice Monday & Thursday) · parking 200 pesos (tablón dice gratis para residentes). Las líneas de laundry room y de la ducha del pool SÍ son verdaderas — no todo lo que dice el manager es mentira, solo 3 datos.

Engagement

  • "A real manager, a real board, three real mistakes — can you catch all three?"

Crecimiento
