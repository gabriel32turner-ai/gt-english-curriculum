Grammar · Don't have to / doesn't have to

13–25 min · Discover → understand → compete
New pointGRM-03 Sentence Auction · subasta
Discover

Read the message. What's different from "have to"?

🏝️🍽️

You have to check in at three.

OK. Do we have to cook?

No! You don't have to cook — there's a buffet. And she doesn't have to pay for the kids.

Perfect, this trip is easy.

Count them. How many do you see?(Respuesta: 2 · don't have to, doesn't have to)
The tool

Don't / doesn't have to = not necessary

1 · I / you / we / they

don't have to + verb

We don't have to bring towels.

2 · he / she / it

doesn't have to + verb

She doesn't have to pay.

3 · The meaning

not necessary — NOT prohibited

You don't have to wear a hat = you can wear it or not.

Sentence
I/you/we/theyWe don't have to bring towels. · They don't have to book a table.
he/she/itShe doesn't have to pay. · The hotel doesn't have to close the pool early.

Don't / doesn't have to = no hace falta, no es obligatorio — NO es "prohibido". Con he / she: doesn't have to. Después siempre va el verbo base. Solo reconocimiento hoy: "Do you have to pay? — No, you don't." se practica a fondo en la Sesión 19.

¿Qué es? Don't / doesn't have to significa "no hace falta" o "no es necesario" — NO significa que algo esté prohibido.

¿Cómo se usa? Con I / you / we / they: don't have to. Con he / she / it: doesn't have to. Después siempre va el verbo en forma base: doesn't have to pay (nunca "pays").

¿Cómo suena? doesn't se pronuncia /ˈdʌzənt/ (con sonido "z"); don't have to se pronuncia /doʊnt hæf tə/ — la "v" de have suena como "f". El acento cae en la palabra que nombra el servicio: COOK, PAY, TOWELS.

¿Para qué hoy? Para describir lo que un viaje todo incluido te quita de encima — lo que necesitas en la Misión.

Practice A · Mechanical

Choose: don't have to · doesn't have to

Practice B · GRM-03

Sentence Auction — buy only the correct ones

1. Read each sentence out loud. 2. Decide: is it correct? 3. Tap "Buy" only if you think it's right. Buying a correct sentence costs 100; buying a WRONG one costs DOUBLE (200) — read carefully before you bid!

Wallet: 1000
Watch out

Mistakes at this level

She don't have to pay.She doesn't have to pay.he/she/it → doesn't have to, not don't have to.
We doesn't have to cook.We don't have to cook.we/you/they → don't have to.
You don't has to check out early.You don't have to check out early.Base form after "don't" — never "has".
Teacher notes · §3 Grammar

Pedagógica

  • Punto NUEVO del día. Da tiempo real al Discover antes de nombrar la regla. Practice A es mecánica (máx. 1/3 del bloque).
  • Sentence Auction es COMUNICATIVA: el alumno debe leer y juzgar la oración ANTES de comprar — no compres sin que diga por qué cree que está bien o mal.
  • Nivel: solo la forma negativa (don't/doesn't have to). La forma con "?" (Do/Does...have to?) es solo reconocimiento hoy — se practica a fondo en S19. Nunca introduzcas mustn't ni needn't.

Engagement

  • "Buy smart — a wrong sentence costs you double."

Crecimiento

  • "Don't have to" regresa en S19 (Do I have to…?), S20 (avisos) y la Mission de S32.

Your mission

Design an all-inclusive weekend package for one of these guests. Say 3 things they HAVE to do and 4 things they DON'T have to do.
