Grammar · Should / Shouldn't

13–25 min · Discover → understand → auction
New pointGRM-03 Sentence Auction · subasta
Discover

Read the chat. How do we give advice?

💡📞

You should call your mom back.

I know. I shouldn't wait much longer.

Should I call her right now?

Yes, you should. Do it today!

Count them. How many do you see?(Respuesta: 4 · You should, I shouldn't, Should I, you should)
The tool

Subject + should/shouldn't + verb (base)

1 · Affirmative

Subject + should + verb

You should call her. He should rest. We should leave early.

2 · Negative

Subject + shouldn't + verb

You shouldn't eat late. They shouldn't wait.

3 · Question + short answer

Should + subject + verb?

Should I go? — Yes, you should. / No, you shouldn't.

Form → Example
Affirmativesubject + should + verb — You should call her. He should rest.
Negativesubject + shouldn't + verb — You shouldn't eat late. They shouldn't wait.
QuestionShould I go? — Yes, you should. / No, you shouldn't.

Should = debería (consejo). Igual para todas las personas: I/you/he/she/we/they should + verbo base (SIN -s). Negativo: shouldn't. Pregunta: Should I…? Es un consejo, no una obligación (distinto de have to).

¿Qué es? Es la forma de DAR UN CONSEJO en inglés.

¿Cómo se usa? Igual para todas las personas: I / you / he / she / we / they + should + verbo base, SIN -s (He should rest, nunca "He shoulds"). Negativo: shouldn't + verbo base. Pregunta: Should + sujeto + verbo? (Should I go?). Respuesta corta: Yes, you should. / No, you shouldn't.

¿Cómo suena? "Should" /ʃʊd/ — la "l" NO se pronuncia. "Shouldn't" /ˈʃʊdnt/. Entonación amable y descendente cuando das un consejo.

¿Para qué hoy? Para dar consejo sobre situaciones cotidianas — lo vas a usar en la Mission de hoy.

Practice A · Mechanical

Choose the correct form

Practice B · GRM-03

Sentence Auction — buy only the correct sentences

1. You have 10 chips. 2. Tap "Buy" on a sentence only if you think it's correct. 3. A correct sentence costs 1 chip. A wrong sentence costs DOUBLE — 2 chips. Spend wisely!

Chips: 100 / 8 decided

Watch out

Mistakes at this level

You should to call.You should call.Never use "to" after should — should + verb base, directly.
She shoulds rest.She should rest.Should NEVER takes -s, not even with he/she/it.
He should not to wait.He shouldn't wait.Shouldn't = should not. Never add "to" after it either.
Teacher notes · §3 Grammar

Pedagógica

  • Punto NUEVO del día. Da tiempo real al Discover antes de nombrar la regla. Practice A es mecánica (máx. 1/3 del bloque).
  • Sentence Auction es SUBASTA: el alumno arriesga fichas comprando solo lo que cree correcto; comprar mal cuesta el doble — el costo enseña a leer con cuidado, no a adivinar rápido.
  • Nivel: solo consejo directo. Sin ought to / had better / If I were you. Sin consejos médicos reales — solo vida cotidiana ficticia.

Engagement

  • "Ten chips. Spend them like a smart shopper — buy only what you're sure is correct."

Crecimiento

DEAR ADVICE COLUMN

Q1: My brother doesn't save money. Now he needs two thousand pesos. Should I give him the money?

Q2: My best friend wants to quit her job. She doesn't ask for advice, but I think it's a bad idea.

Q3: My mom gives me advice about everything, even small things. I love her, but it's too much.

ADVICE: For Q1, you should wait and ask him to plan first. For Q2, you shouldn't give advice when nobody asks — just listen. For Q3, try this: "Thanks, Mom. I'm going to think about it." You don't have to follow every idea.

Teacher notes · §5 Listening

Pedagógica

  • Guion de 2 hablantes, para grabar: LARA (mujer, British English, lenta) y TOM (hombre, British English, lento). Nota de continuidad: este TOM es un personaje NUEVO y DISTINTO del TOM de S06/S16, que es US English nativo — no reutilizar esa voz aquí; en el archivo de audio la columna de voz lo aclara explícitamente para quien grabe.
  • Predict and Score (APUESTA): el alumno arriesga su predicción ANTES de escuchar — esto obliga a procesar el problema de Lara con lógica, no solo a oír.
  • Answer key — Global: (a) advice. Should (elige 2 de 3): go to bed earlier · buy an alarm clock (far from the bed); "try tea" es el distractor (Tom lo dice como orden simple, sin "should"). Shouldn't (elige 2 de 3): use the phone in bed · drink coffee after six; "drink coffee in the morning" es el distractor.

Teacher notes · §7 Mission

Pedagógica

  • Las 3 tarjetas de amigo son 100% ficticias — el alumno NUNCA tiene que dar consejo sobre su vida real. Dado el riesgo "Spicy" del tema (consejos no solicitados), recálcalo antes de empezar: ninguna situación de la Mission pide datos reales del alumno o de su familia.
  • Umbral de éxito: 6 consejos correctos en total (4 should + 2 shouldn't) de los 9 campos disponibles (3 amigos × 2 should + 1 shouldn't) — no es necesario llenar los 9 a la perfección, basta alcanzar el mínimo.
  • El "Should I…?" de cada amigo es texto fijo (simulado), no requiere respuesta del alumno ahí — solo informa su consejo final.

Engagement

  • Anuncia el resultado en voz alta: "6 correct pieces of advice + a clear decision — Advice Table complete!"

Crecimiento

  • "In Session 27 you are going to add a reason to every piece of advice: I think you should… because…"
