Grammar · Do I have to…? / Does she have to…?

13–25 min · Discover → understand → compete
New pointGRM-06 Interrogation · ocultamiento
Discover

Read the chat. How do we ask with "have to"?

🏋️🪪

Do I have to pay today?

No, you don't. You have to pay on Monday.

OK. Does she have to sign a form too?

Yes, she does. Everybody signs on the first day.

Count them. How many do you see?(Respuesta: 4 · Do I have to, No you don't, Does she have to, Yes she does)
The tool

Do / Does + subject + have to + verb?

1 · I / you / we / they

Do + subject + have to + verb?

Do you have to pay? — Yes, I do. / No, I don't.

2 · he / she / it

Does + subject + have to + verb?

Does she have to sign? — Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't.

3 · Short answers

Yes, I do. · No, he doesn't.

Never repeat "have to" in the short answer.

Question → Short answer
I/you/we/theyDo I have to pay? — Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
he/she/itDoes she have to sign? — Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't.

Pregunta: Do / Does + sujeto + have to + verbo base (Do I have to pay?). Respuesta corta: Yes, you do. / No, you don't. Con he / she: Does he have to…? — Yes, he does. / No, he doesn't.

¿Qué es? Es la forma de PREGUNTAR si algo es obligatorio, usando have to.

¿Cómo se usa? Con I / you / we / they: Do + sujeto + have to + verbo base (Do we have to pay?). Con he / she / it: Does + sujeto + have to + verbo base (Does he have to sign?). La respuesta corta NUNCA repite "have to": Yes, you do. / No, she doesn't.

¿Cómo suena? "Do I" se enlaza /duː aɪ/, con entonación ascendente ↗ al final de la pregunta. "Have to" se pronuncia /hæf tə/ — la "v" suena como "f".

¿Para qué hoy? Para preguntar las reglas de un lugar nuevo antes de inscribirte — lo vas a usar en la Mission de hoy.

Teacher notes · §3 Grammar

Pedagógica

  • Punto NUEVO del día. Da tiempo real al Discover antes de nombrar la regla. Practice A es mecánica (máx. 1/3 del bloque).
  • Interrogation es OCULTAMIENTO: el maestro (o una tarjeta) tiene la regla; el alumno pregunta SOLO con Do I / Do we / Does he have to…? Menos preguntas es mejor — aquí la tarjeta ya revela 4 reglas, una por botón, para que la corrida desatendida sea jugable sin un compañero presente.
  • Nivel: no introduzcas must ni needn't. Yes/No do/does — nunca "Yes, I have".

Engagement

  • "Ask smart — can you find all 4 rules without wasting a question?"

Crecimiento

  • Do I have to regresa en S20 y S24 y como pregunta de aclaración en S30.

Teacher notes · §5 Listening

Pedagógica
