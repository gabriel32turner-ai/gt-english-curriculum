Where were you at seven?

No hay un punto nuevo — hoy los tres sistemas se usan JUNTOS.(Respuesta: was/were · did/didn't · there was/were)
The tool

Three systems, one story

Was / Were

I was · you were

I was home. We were outside. Were you there?

Did / Didn't

did + base · didn't + base

Did you go? I didn't go. He didn't come until eight.

There was / were

there was + sing. · there were + pl.

There was a noise. There were people outside.

¿Qué es? No hay gramática nueva: se junta lo que ya se vio de S5 a S11 — was/were, verbos regulares e irregulares en pasado, did/didn't, there was/there were.

¿Cómo se usa? Was/were describe estados (I was, there was a noise). Did/didn't pregunta y niega acciones (Did you go? I didn't go). There was/were describe que algo existió (there was a loud noise, there were many people).

¿Cómo suena? La t final de wasn't y didn't se pronuncia clara. Linking: went‿out, came‿back se enlazan como una sola palabra.

¿Para qué hoy? Para conectar los datos de 4 vecinos distintos en un solo relato del apagón.

Watch out

Mistakes at this level

The lights was out.The lights were out.Plural subject → were, not was.
We didn't went outside.We didn't go outside.didn't + base form, not the past form.
There was many neighbors outside.There were many neighbors outside.many neighbors is plural → there were.
