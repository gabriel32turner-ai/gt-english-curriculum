Grammar · should + because

13–25 min · Discover → understand → compete
New pointGRM-10 Reverse Engineering · ocultamiento · 2ª aparición
Discover

Read the chat. How do we ask for advice — and give it?

💭📱

I have a dilemma. What should I do? My phone is old.

I think you should wait, because the battery is still OK.

But the camera is bad. What should I do?

Then I think you should look for a cheap phone. You shouldn't buy it online, because you can't try it first.

Count them. How many do you see?(Respuesta: 5 · What should I do, I think you should, because, I think you should, You shouldn't)
The tool

I think you should… + because + a short reason

1 · Asking for advice

What should I do? · Should I buy it?

What should I do? Should we go by bus?

2 · Giving advice (+)

I think you should + verb + because…

I think you should wait because the price is high.

3 · Giving advice (−)

You shouldn't + verb + because…

You shouldn't buy it because it's old.

Example
+I think you should wait because the price is high.
−You shouldn't buy it because it's old.
?What should I do? Should we go by bus?

"What should I do?" = ¿qué debería hacer? Para responder: "I think you should… + because + una razón corta." Una sola razón por consejo.

¿Qué es? "Should" sirve para dar o pedir un CONSEJO — una recomendación, no una obligación.

¿Cómo se usa? Para pedir consejo: What should I do? / Should I buy it? Para dar un consejo positivo: I think you should + verbo base + because + razón corta (I think you should wait because the price is high). Para un consejo negativo: You shouldn't + verbo base + because (You shouldn't buy it because it's old). Nunca "should to" ni "should + verbo con -s".

¿Cómo suena? El énfasis cae en SHOULD: "I think you SHOULD wait". "Because" se pronuncia débil, casi como /bɪkəz/, nunca remarcado. La razón, después de "because", baja la voz al final ↘.

¿Para qué hoy? Para pedir y dar consejos simples con una razón — lo vas a usar en la Mission de hoy con "The Dilemma Panel".

Practice A · Mechanical

Choose: should · shouldn't

Practice B · GRM-10

Reverse Engineering — what was the question?

1. The teacher gives the ANSWER — the full piece of advice. 2. You write the question that caused it. 3. Use "should" and a key word from the advice (what / buy / etc.). Check your question below.

Questions written: 0 / 3
Watch out

Mistakes at this level

You should to wait.You should wait.After should/shouldn't always the base verb — never "to".
She should buys it.She should buy it.Base form after should — never add -s.
He should not to buy it.He shouldn't buy it.shouldn't = should not. Never "not to".
Teacher notes · §3 Grammar

Pedagógica

  • Punto NUEVO del día: should + because. Da tiempo real al Discover antes de nombrar la regla. Practice A es mecánica (máx. 1/3 del bloque).
  • Reverse Engineering es OCULTAMIENTO, 2ª aparición del nivel (la 1ª fue S01/S14): se muestra el CONSEJO completo y el alumno produce la PREGUNTA que lo provocó. Verificación flexible por palabras clave (should + what/do, should + buy, should + buy/online) para que la corrida desatendida sea evaluable sin depender de un compañero.
  • Nivel: solo una razón simple por consejo. Sin condicionales (If I were you… queda fuera del nivel) y sin análisis extenso de consecuencias.
  • Recicla "because" (S15) y "should" (S26) — hoy se combinan por primera vez.

Engagement

  • "I give you the advice — can you guess the exact question that caused it?"

Crecimiento

  • should + because regresa en S28 y S32.

Pedagógica

IMPORTANTE: este MARK es un personaje DISTINTO del MARK que aparecerá en Session 31 (ahí MARK es German English) — son dos personajes con el mismo nombre de pila, documentado también en el archivo de audio para no confundir voice_id al grabar.

Teacher notes · §6 Speaking

Pedagógica

  • Choose One: The Adult Dilemma, 2ª aparición del nivel (la 1ª fue S17): aquí el alumno NO elige su postura — la tarjeta se la asigna al azar (rol oculto/forzado) y debe defenderla igual, aunque no sea su opinión real. Mission · The Dilemma Panel

Real-life taskEvidence
Your mission

Present your dilemma to the panel: "Buy a used phone now, or wait and save?" Get a recommendation from each advisor, ask 2 follow-up questions, then decide.

Success: 3 recommendations with should + because + 1 question "What should I do?" + a final decision in 2 sentences.

Group: in pairs, one person presents the dilemma; the other plays all 3 advisors out loud, one voice at a time, before writing anything down.

1:1: the teacher plays the 3 advisors out loud with 3 different voices; the student writes down each recommendation.

Teacher notes · §7 Mission

Pedagógica

  • The Dilemma Panel: los 3 "asesores" son simulados — el mismo alumno escribe la recomendación de cada uno con una voz/perspectiva distinta (The Saver, The Tech Friend, The Practical One), practicando should + because tres veces desde ángulos diferentes.
  • missionCheck() usa regex: exige "should" + "because" en las 3 tarjetas de asesor, la pregunta inicial con "should", las 2 preguntas de seguimiento no vacías, y la decisión final con 2 oraciones como mínimo.
  • El dilema es 100% ficticio — nadie necesita describir una compra real propia.

Engagement

  • Anuncia el resultado en voz alta: "3 pieces of advice + a real decision — who convinced you the most?"

Crecimiento

  • "In Session 28 you are going to open a clinic and give advice to real situations, one after another."
