§2 Speaking — Full Integration Role-Play 10 min
💡 ENGAGEMENT NOTE — THE FINAL ROLE-PLAY ▼
This is the last speaking practice before the Exit Project. Keep stakes real: "This is your final practice. The Exit Project is the same format — a conversation with me, your manager, about a real project. Use what you know." Resist the urge to over-correct. Note errors quietly and address in the group debrief after all pairs finish.
T-S
Valeria presents the full Apex project to the regional director for the first time. She needs to cover: what happened in M6 / what the team is planning / what commitments she makes right now. You are the director — ask three questions across all tenses after the presentation.

Opening: "Good morning. I'm going to give you a full update on the Apex project."
Past: "Last quarter we had some delays, but we resolved them. We completed the quality audit and delivered the Phase 1 report on time."
Plans: "Next quarter we're going to start Phase 2 and add a new supplier from Guadalajara."
Commitment: "I'll send you the full summary today, and I'll keep you posted on any changes."
Closing: "Do you have any questions?"

S-S
Use your OWN real project from work. Partner A: manager, asks 5 questions across all three tenses — must include at least one Wh- question per tense. Partner B: answers fully, adds proactive information, uses at least 3 colloquial phrases from any session. Switch roles after 2 minutes.

A: "What did you accomplish last month?" B: "Last month we implemented the new quality procedure. We're back on track after the delay." A: "What are you going to do about the budget?" B: "We're going to renegotiate next week — and to be honest, I think we can save 10%." A: "What will you do today?" B: "I'll send you the updated cost report. I'll keep you posted."

Class
Class vote on the best performance: which pair used all three tenses most naturally? Which pair used the most colloquial phrases? Which pair sounded most like a real professional conversation? Vote on fluency only — no grammar police.

Look for: natural tense switching without pausing, use of fillers ("To be honest" / "Let me think"), proactive volunteering of information, correct spontaneous/planned distinction, at least 3 colloquial phrases. These are the Exit Project rubric criteria.

§5 Grammar Review — The Full A2.1 Tense System 12 min
⚡ TEACHER NOTE — GRAMMAR REVIEW STRATEGY ▼
Don't re-teach. Frame it as confirmation: "You already know all of this. Let's confirm it." Run Activity A as a self-check — students compare their answers in pairs before you reveal. Activity B (error correction) uses real errors collected from M6 sessions; if you noted student errors, substitute them here. This is always the most memorable review.
TenseFormWh- QuestionTypical Signal Words
Simple Past V-ed / irregular · did/didn't What did you do? yesterday · last week · last month · ago · in [year]
Going To am/is/are + going to + base What are you going to do? next week · next month · on Friday · this quarter · soon
Will will + base / won't + base What will you do? right now · today · this afternoon · immediately · I promise
ACTIVITY A — ERROR CORRECTION: Fix the Tense
1. "Tomorrow I will present the Q2 results — I already confirmed it with Daniel last week."
2. "Last week, we didn't received the components on time."
3. "The meeting is finishing — let me think about that for a second. I will handle the report." (spontaneous)
4. "What you did last week with the supplier?" (question)
5. "We are going to presented the results on Friday — it's confirmed."
ACTIVITY B — REORDER: The Complete Exit Email

Put these parts of a three-tense business email in the correct order.

  • ⠿I will send you the final project report by end of Friday. Please find the summary attached.
  • ⠿Next quarter, we are going to launch Phase 2 and add a second supplier from Guadalajara.
  • ⠿Dear Mr. Reyes, I hope you are doing well. I am writing to give you a full update on the Apex project.
  • ⠿Last quarter was challenging but productive. We resolved the supplier delay and completed the quality audit on schedule.
  • ⠿I look forward to hearing from you. Best regards, Valeria Torres.
§6 Listening — Valeria's Full Module 6 Report 10 min
⚡ TEACHER NOTE — INTEGRATED LISTENING TASK ▼
This is the longest listening of A2.1 — ~185 words, approximately 60 seconds at speed 0.82. It integrates every grammar structure and colloquial phrase from Module 6. Use it as a final benchmark: if students can follow this script with 80%+ comprehension, they are ready to exit A2.1.
Valeria's Full Module 6 Report — Solaris Manufacturing, Celaya
A2.1 · M6 · S5 · Voice: Valeria (Mexican accent) · Speed: 0.82 · ~60 sec
Good morning, everyone. I want to give you a complete summary of the Apex project — everything we did, what we're planning, and what I'll do next. Let me start with the past. Last quarter was difficult. We had three supplier delays, two quality issues, and one budget overrun. But we didn't give up. We resolved every problem — we worked extra hours, we renegotiated the contract, and we added a second supplier from Guadalajara. By the end of the quarter, we were back on track. We also completed the quality audit and delivered the Phase 1 report on time. The outcome was very positive. Now, looking ahead. Next quarter, we are going to start Phase 2 of the integration. We are also going to present the new risk management plan to all stakeholders. I already scheduled that meeting — it's on the 18th. And we're going to implement a new communication protocol to keep everyone in the loop. Finally, my immediate commitments. I will send you the full Phase 2 scope document today. I will also copy Daniel on all future supplier communications. And I will call the Guadalajara supplier this afternoon to confirm their capacity for Q3. To be honest, I'm proud of what this team accomplished. Are we on the same page? Let's move forward together. Thank you.

TRUE / FALSE

1. The team had three supplier delays and two quality issues last quarter.
2. The team didn't complete the Phase 1 report.
3. The stakeholder meeting is already scheduled for the 18th.
4. Valeria will contact the Guadalajara supplier next week.
5. Valeria used at least 3 colloquial phrases from Module 6 in her report.

No notes allowed. Natural, professional English.
