🎭 Main oral activity · 3 progressive rounds · Describe real people from your work with adj + very/quite + adj+noun
⏱ 12–15 MIN
🎭 Round 1 — Guiada (scaffold visible)
💬 "What's your team like?"
Your new colleague in Chicago wants to know what the people on your team are like. Scaffold visible. Describe 2 people. Minimum 4 sentences per person.
👔 Teacher (new U.S. colleague)
Jake Miller · Chicago
Empieza: "Hey! I'm the new sales rep. What are the people in your team like? Is your manager easy to work with? And who else is on your team?"
🗣️ Student
Tu nombre · Tu empresa
Describe your manager + 1 colleague. Use adj + very/quite + adj+noun. 4+ sentences per person. Scaffold visible.
Scaffold Round 1:
"My manager is very ___ and quite ___. She/He is a very ___ ___."
"My colleague [name] is quite ___ but very ___. She/He is also very ___."
"She/He has ___ hair and is always very ___ in meetings."
🎭 Round 2 — Semi-libre (solo preguntas dthe teacher)
💬 Segunda llamada — without scaffold
Misma situación. Without scaffold. The teacher guía con preguntas específicas. The student responde con oraciones completas.
👔 Maestro — preguntas disponibles
Prompts para the teacher
"What's your manager like?" · "Is she/he organized?" · "Who's the most creative on your team?" · "Is there a shy person?" · "Who's the funniest?" · "What's your supervisor like?"
🗣️ Alumno — libre
Without scaffold
Respuestas de 2–3 oraciones. "She's very ___ — a very ___ ___. She also has ___ hair and she's quite ___."
⭐ Round 3 — Libre (Challenge: 90 segundos)
🎤 Full Team Description
90 seconds. Without the teacher as interlocutor. The student describes their full team using at least 8 adjectives from S1A, very/quite at least 4 times, and adj+noun at least 2 times.
🏆 Goal: 8+ adj · 4+ very/quite · 2+ adj+noun = ⭐ Excellent · 5–7 adj · 2–3 very/quite = ✅ Good · fewer = 📋 Practice describing people at home
Layer 1 · Pedagogical
  • ACTION: Round 1 = fluid with scaffold. Round 2 = the teacher asks varied questions. Round 3 = timer in hand.
  • Immediate recast in Rounds 2 and 3: if the student omits very/quite → say the same sentence with very/quite and ask "Do you hear the difference?"
  • Round 3 goal: 90 seconds without stopping. If they stop for more than 3 seconds → give a prompting word: "What about your colleague? Is she/he creative?"
Layer 2 · Engagement
  • "In Round 1 you needed the scaffold. In Round 3 you speak 90 seconds without stopping. That is what happened in 30 minutes of class." 💪
Layer 3 · Retention
  • "§5 is the Personality Bingo — the most entertaining game in M3. Exactly the adjectives you used in the role play will appear there."

Layer 1 · Pedagogical
  • ACTION: Read the clues OUT LOUD — do not show the adjective name yet. The student must identify the adjective and click. Then click the clue to verify.
  • Competitive variation: the teacher also has a mental board. The first to complete 4 in a row says "BINGO!" — creates tension and energy.
  • Production variation: after marking the adjective, the student makes ONE sentence with that adj about their real work. Double productivity.
  • The clues can be read in any order. Mix the easy ones (P1, P10) with the harder ones (P7, P11) to keep the pace.
Layer 2 · Engagement

Layer 3 · Retention

§ 06
Debate — What is the ideal manager?
💬 Free oral production · 3 debate questions · Use S1A adjectives to defend your position · No correct answer — just real English
⏱ 8–10 MIN
💬 Question 1 — The ideal manager
What's the ideal personality for a manager? Which 3 adjectives from S1A are most important?
Defend your position: "A good manager should be very ___ because ___. She also needs to be ___ when ___. I think ___ is not important because ___."
🟢 Team A: organized + serious + confident → Why?
🔴 Team B: friendly + patient + easygoing → Why?
💬 Question 2 — The perfect colleague
Your new colleague can only have ONE personality trait. Which one would you choose and why?
"I would choose ___ because in my work we need someone who ___. A ___ colleague is better than a ___ one because ___."
🟢 hardworking → Gets things done
🔴 easygoing → Better work environment
⭐ Question 3 — Real talk about your work
Describe the real personality of someone at YOUR work — without saying their name. Your partner guesses who it is.
"This person is very ___ and quite ___. She/He has ___ hair. She/He is always ___ in meetings. She/He is a very ___ ___. Who is it?"
Layer 1 · Pedagogical
  • ACTION: Debate 1 and 2 — assign opposing positions. The student defends theirs even if they disagree. This forces creative production.
  • Debate 3 (Guess Who): this is the most natural and fun game. If the student describes well — the teacher guesses. Then switch roles: the teacher describes → student guesses.
  • Check adj+noun in Debate 3: "She's a very ___ ___" — the noun must come after the adj.
  • If the student does not know how to defend → give options: "Do you prefer organized or easygoing? Why?" — questions in Spanish if needed to unblock them.
Layer 2 · Engagement
  • "There is debate because the adjectives are ALREADY in your active vocabulary. If you can defend a position in English — that is fluency, not just accuracy." 🔥
Layer 3 · Retention
  • "In S2B the Possession Spinner combines S1A adj + possessive 's from S2A. Today you consolidate the adj so that S2B is pure combined production."
