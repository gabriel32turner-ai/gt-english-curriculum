📘 How to run Hot Take Give the student 20 seconds to look at the screen time number and react silently, then start the timer. The key requirement: use passive voice at least once — "This time was spent on…" / "My attention was taken by…" / "I was kept awake by…". Listen for the missing "be" verb error ("This time spent on…" — catch it, correct it after the timer ends). The goal is authentic, spontaneous production. No grammar input at this stage — pure output under pressure.
§2 Pitch Round — Assigned Sides Two assigned sides · Passive voice arguments · 90 sec per side ⏱ 10 MIN
💬 Motion: "Companies should be legally required to limit how long users can spend on their apps each day." — Assign sides. Push for passive voice: "Time limits should be set by law." / "Users should not be controlled by government regulations."
Side A — Pro Limits
"Time limits should be set by law"
  • "Screen time addiction was created by these companies deliberately…"
  • "Young people's mental health is being damaged by excessive usage…"
  • "Daily limits should be imposed to protect vulnerable users…"
Side B — Anti Limits
"Personal freedom should not be removed"
  • "Adults should not be told how long they can use their own apps…"
  • "If limits are enforced by governments, personal freedom is reduced…"
  • "The responsibility should be carried by users, not regulated away…"
💡 Engagement tip After both sides, flip the question: "What's YOUR real position?" Ask them to state it in one passive sentence: "I think daily limits should be set because…" or "I think this decision should be left to the individual because…" This converts the debate into genuine opinion-forming. Push for the passive construction — it forces a considered, analytical tone rather than a raw opinion, which is the real B1.1 production goal.
§3 Relay — Transform It 5 transformations · Active ↔ Passive · Error detection · Personal response ⏱ 8 MIN
🔄 How Transform It works: Each card shows a sentence to transform — active to passive, passive to active, tense change, or error fix. The student responds orally. Click "Show Answer" to reveal the model. Card 5 is always personal — no single correct answer.
1
Active → Passive
"This app tracks your location 24/7."
Your location is tracked by this app 24/7.
2
Passive → Active
"Screen time data is collected by your phone automatically."
Your phone collects screen time data automatically.
3
Present → Past Passive
"App limits are bypassed every day."
App limits were bypassed every day.
4
Fix the Error
"My sleep improved by reducing screen time."
My sleep was improved by reducing screen time. — The passive verb "was" is missing! Passive voice always needs: be + past participle. "improved" alone is active past tense, not passive.
5
Your Reality
"Rodrigo said his habits were changed by one month without Instagram. What was changed in YOUR habits this week?"
Open response — student answers with: "My [habit] was changed when I…" — no single correct answer. Listen for passive voice structure and encourage specific, honest personal content. Write their sentence down verbatim — it becomes a model for §5 Solo.
📘 Facilitation note For cards 1–4, push for speed — the transformation should come in 3–5 seconds. If the student hesitates on card 4, the error is subtle: "improved" looks right but it's active past tense, not passive. Ask: "Does this sentence have a 'be' verb?" That question alone usually unlocks the correction. Card 5 deserves 20–30 seconds. The personal connection is the most important moment of this relay.
§4 Speed Round — Screen Time Grammar 10 cards · Passive voice in screen time context · Score 7+ for badge ⏱ 6 MIN
Score 0 / 10
1. My sleep ______ by late-night phone use.
2. App notifications ______ every 15 minutes to bring you back.
3. The 24-hour limit ______ within 10 minutes of setting it.
4. Screen time data ______ automatically and stored in the cloud.
5. This app ______ to make you spend as much time as possible inside it.
6. My focus ______ by years of constant multitasking on my phone.
7. Wellness features ______ by Apple and Google after public pressure.
8. These habits ______ over years, not days.
9. Thousands of user reviews ______ to improve the app.
10. If screens ______ from bedrooms, sleep quality improves significantly.
📘 Speed Round note Run this as call-and-respond — student says the answer aloud before clicking. Score 7 or more earns the Speed badge ⚡. Card 4 is a common trap: "collects" is tempting because the sentence reads naturally, but data is the subject receiving the action, not doing it. Card 6 is the hardest: "shortened" alone looks like past tense (active), not "was shortened" (passive). After the round, ask: "Which one made you hesitate?" — that's the structure to revisit.
§5 Solo Challenge — 2 Minutes 120 seconds · Digital Wellness Audit · 3+ passives · No notes ⏱ 8 MIN
🎤 Solo Challenge — 2 Minutes
Your Digital Wellness Audit — tell the story of your screen time: what habits were formed, how your attention was affected, what you tried to change, and what YOUR plan is going forward. Use at least 3 passive voice sentences.
01
Past habits: "Before, these habits were formed by…" / "My attention was affected by…"
02
Current impact: "My sleep is still being affected by…" / "Time is being wasted on…"
03
Future plan: "From now on, my screen time will be reduced by…" / "Notifications will be turned off when…"
04
Use at least 3 passive voice sentences — teacher tracks each one.
05
No notes. Honest and specific — not generic statements anyone could make.
📘 Assessment Track: (1) Passive count — 3+ passives with correct "be + past participle" structure (0–3 pts), (2) Personal depth — is this their real story, with specific habits and named apps, not a generic answer? (0–4 pts), (3) Structure — past / present / future sections are clear (0–3 pts). Max 10 points. Enter scores in Leaderboard §7. Most common error: "My habits changed" (active past) instead of "My habits were changed" (passive). Note it and correct it immediately after the timer.
§6 Source Analysis — Digital Wellness Language in the Wild 4 source cards · Passive voice in authentic contexts · 4/4 correct earns badge ⏱ 8 MIN
📋 WHO Report Extract
"Screen time of more than 4 hours per day has been linked to higher rates of anxiety and depression in adults. Sleep quality is significantly reduced when devices are used within one hour of bedtime. These findings were published in the Journal of Digital Health in 2023."
What has been linked to 4+ hours of screen time per day?
💬 Tech Blog Comment
"I turned off all notifications except phone calls. My stress was reduced immediately — I didn't realise how much I was being controlled by the constant pinging. My focus was recovered within a week. It was the best productivity decision I've ever made."
What changed when notifications were turned off?
📄 Company Policy Extract
"All employees are encouraged to disconnect from work devices after 8pm. Emails sent after business hours are not expected to be answered until the next working day. Your wellbeing is valued by this company."
What is the company asking employees to do?
🔍 Grammar Detective
"Notifications were turned off at 9pm. My phone is placed on silent mode. Screen time is tracked weekly. Sleep quality was improved after one month."
How many passive sentences are there?
💡 Discussion anchor After the 4 cards, ask: "Which source uses passive voice most strategically — and why?" Best answer target for the company policy: "The passive voice hides the agent — 'are not expected to be answered' sounds like a universal rule, not a direct order from the boss. It's softer and more professional." That's B1.1+ level analysis. For card 4, walk through each sentence: "turned off" → passive ✓, "is placed" → passive ✓, "is tracked" → passive ✓, "was improved" → passive ✓ — all four.

Screen Time & Digital Wellness — Passive Voice in Context
Passive Voice Screen time vocabulary Digital wellness 2-min fluency
🎯 This week at work — use these 3 phrases
3 Phrases Starting Tomorrow
PHRASE 1
"My sleep was improved by turning off my phone at night."
Passive voice in real life. Use it when someone asks about your sleep or energy levels. "My sleep was improved just by leaving my phone in another room." The passive "was improved" places the action on you (result), not on what you did (cause) — sounds analytical and credible. Natural in health, wellbeing, and productivity conversations.
PHRASE 2
"I was being controlled by notifications."
Passive to describe a tech habit. The past continuous passive "was being controlled" shows an ongoing process you were caught in — it's more honest and specific than "I used my phone too much." Use it when talking about productivity, focus, or your relationship with technology. It signals self-awareness at a B1.1+ level.
PHRASE 3
"I need to disconnect."
Digital wellness phrase — simple and powerful. Use it literally (stepping away from devices) or figuratively (taking a mental break from work communication). Pair it with a passive for extra impact: "I need to disconnect — I feel like I'm being pulled back constantly." Works in professional and personal settings. Shows emotional intelligence and awareness of tech habits.
