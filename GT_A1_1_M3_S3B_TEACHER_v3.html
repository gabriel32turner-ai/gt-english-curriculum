Audio Model — Carlos' Zoom Onboarding
🎧 Carlos presenta a su equipo en Zoom usando la Description Chain completa · 3 frases shadowing · Now You presenta tu equipo
⏱ 8–10 MIN
🎙️ Carlos Rivera — Zoom Onboarding (Description Chain completa)
ElevenLabs · speed 0.78 · Escucha completo primero · Luego shadowing ↓

🔊 Escucha cada frase → repite out loud → escucha de nuevo. Pon atención a los 4 pasos del Chain y a la fórmula Zoom. 3 rondas mínimo.

🔊 Shadowing — Las 3 Frases Modelo de S3A
Phrase 1 = Zoom formula. Phrase 2 = adj + possessive. Phrase 3 = What's she like? + answer. Full Chain in 3 phrases.
1
"Everyone, I'd like you all to meet Ana — she's our new Production Coordinator."
2
"She's very organized — and her reports are always excellent."
3
"What's she like? She's friendly but quite serious at work."
🎙️ Now You — Mismo Formato que Carlos
Introduce 1 real person from your work on Zoom — just like Carlos. Use the formula "I'd like you all to meet" + the 4 Chain steps. Without notes.
➡ "Everyone, I'd like you all to meet ___. She/He's our ___. She/He's very ___ and quite ___. ___'s ___ is always ___."
Layer 1 · Pedagogical
  • ACTION: In the "Now You" time it for 30 seconds. If the student says the full Chain in 30 sec → ⭐. If not → identify which step was missing.
  • Phrase 3 (What's she like?) — practice the Q&A. You ask → student answers about their real colleague. 3 rounds.
  • Verify the possessive in Chain step 4: "___'s ___ is ___". If the student omits it → ask: "And what about something that belongs to that person at your work?"
Layer 2 · Engagement
  • "Carlos introduced Ana in 30 seconds — without reading, naturally. You just did the same with your real colleague. That is M3 in action." 🚀
Layer 3 · Retención
  • "§4 is the full onboarding — 3 people, 3 formulas, 2 minutes. The audio was the model. In §4 you are the host."

Role Play — Zoom Onboarding
🎭 The most realistic role play in M3 · You are the host · You introduce your team on Zoom · 3 progressive rounds · M3 Full Combo
⏱ 12–15 MIN
🎭 Round 1 — Guiada (scaffold visible)
💻 "Welcome to the team!" — Zoom Onboarding
You are the host of a Zoom call. 8 people on screen including 3 clients from Chicago. Your manager asks you to introduce 2 people from your team. Scaffold visible. At minimum a full Chain for each person.
👔 Maestro (cliente Chicago)
Sarah Kim · Chicago
After each introduction ask: "What's she/he like?" · "What does she/he do exactly?" · "Nice to meet you!" [student answers for the person]
🎙️ Alumno (host del Zoom)
Tú · Host
Introduce 2 real people from your work. Full Chain (4 steps) for each one. Use Zoom formula + adj + 's. Scaffold visible.
Scaffold Round 1:
"Everyone, I'd like you all to meet ___." · "She/He's our ___." · "She/He's very ___ and quite ___." · "___'s ___ is always ___."
Para responder "What's she like?": "She's very ___ — a very ___ ___."
🎭 Round 2 — Semi-libre (3 fórmulas distintas)
💻 3 personas · 3 fórmulas distintas
Without scaffold. Introduce 3 people using a different formula for each: informal (This is) + formal (I'd like to introduce) + digital (I'd like you all to meet). The teacher checks which formula is used.
👔 Maestro — verificación
Observador de fórmulas
After each intro: "Which formula did you use?" · "Is it the right one for this context?" · If wrong → "Which would be the formal/digital/informal one?"
🎙️ Alumno — libre
Without scaffold
3 people · 3 different formulas · full Chain · adj + 's for each one. Justify the chosen formula.
⭐ Round 3 — Libre (Challenge: 2 minutos)
🎤 Full Team Onboarding Zoom
2 minutes. You are the host. You introduce your complete real team — manager + colleague + 1 more person. 3 different formulas · full Chain (4 steps) · 3+ S1A adj · 2+ possessive 's from S2A. Without notes.
🏆 3 fórmulas distintas · Chain × 3 · 3+ adj · 2+ 's = ⭐ Excelente · 2 fórmulas · Chain × 2 = ✅ Bien · menos = 📋 Practica el onboarding en casa esta semana
Layer 1 · Pedagogical
  • ACTION: Round 2 — after each intro, you ask "What's she/he like?" to activate Chain step 3 naturally.
  • Round 3: visible timer. The student knows they have 2 minutes. This creates real urgency and activates automatic memory.
  • Check that they do not repeat the same formula in Round 2. If they repeat → pause: "Can you use a different formula for this person?"
Layer 2 · Engagement
  • "In Round 1 you needed the scaffold. In Round 3 you introduce 3 people in 2 minutes. That is what makes GT different." 🏆
Layer 3 · Retención
  • "§5 is the Intro Challenge Spinner — faster and more competitive. The role play was the rehearsal; §5 is the speed test with scoring."

§ 06
Debate — La Primera Impresión en Inglés
💬 Free oral production · 3 debate questions · Use intro formulas + adj + Chain to defend your position
⏱ 8–10 MIN
💬 Pregunta 1 — ¿Importa la primera impresión?
Is the first introduction in English important for your job? What happens when you introduce someone badly?
"In my job, the first introduction is very ___ because ___. I think a good introduction should include ___. If you introduce someone badly in English, ___."
🟢 Very important → First impressions last forever
🔴 Not that important → Results matter more
💬 Pregunta 2 — Formal vs Informal
Is formal English always better for introductions? When is "This is Ana" better than "I'd like to introduce Ana García"?
"I think formal English is better when ___. But informal is better when ___. In my company, we usually use ___ because our culture is ___."
⭐ Pregunta 3 — Describe sin nombre
Describe ONE person from your work (without saying their name or role) using only personality adjectives and appearance. Your partner guesses who it is.
"This person is very ___ and quite ___. She/He is a ___ person — always ___ in meetings. She/He has ___ hair and is always very ___. She/He is in charge of ___. Who is it?"
Layer 1 · Pedagogical
  • ACTION: Debate 2 (Formal vs Informal) — forces the student to think about context. More valuable than producing the formula → is producing the DECISION of which one to use.
  • Debate 3 (Describe without name): switch roles too. The teacher describes someone from the student's company (based on what they already know) → student guesses.
Layer 2 · Engagement
  • "You just debated about formal vs informal introductions IN ENGLISH. That is exactly the conversation that American teams have in their orientation." 🌐
Layer 3 · Retención
  • "In S4B you combine intro formulas + to be neg + adj. Today you consolidate the formulas alone. The next step is all of M3 together."
