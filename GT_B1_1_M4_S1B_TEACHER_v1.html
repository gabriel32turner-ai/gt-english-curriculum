§1

🔥 Hot Take — Social Media & Passive Voice

5 min
One opinion. 60 seconds. Passive voice required.
🔥 Hot Take
"Social media was not designed to connect people — it was designed to control them. We are all being manipulated every time we scroll."
Rule 1: Agree OR disagree — pick a side Rule 2: Use passive voice at least twice Rule 3: 60 seconds — no notes Rule 4: Use 1 word from S1A vocabulary
Model Answer — Disagree

"I disagree — but only partly. Social media was designed to connect people, and it does. I've reconnected with people I lost contact with. But yes, over time, the algorithm was built to maximize engagement, not wellbeing. So the original design may have been good, but it has been changed by commercial pressure. It's a curated version of connection — real, but not the full picture."
■ Vocabulary ■ Passive Voice ■ Colloquial
Tip: After the model answer, ask: "¿Cuántas veces usó el pasivo?" Count together. Target: students catch at least 3 passive structures. Then ask who agrees/disagrees — hands up. Use the split to set up the debate in §2.
§2

⚡ Pitch / Debate — Platform Responsibility

10 min
Two sides. Equal time. Use the language you were taught.
🟣 Side A — Companies Are Responsible
"These platforms were designed to be addictive. Users are being manipulated. Regulation is needed."
  • These apps were built to maximize time on screen — not wellbeing.
  • Personal data is collected without users really understanding what they agree to.
  • Young people's self-esteem is damaged by comparison culture online.
  • Dark patterns are used to keep users engaged against their interests.
  • If companies are regulated, users will be better protected.
🟢 Side B — Users Are Responsible
"Users choose what is consumed and shared. Personal responsibility cannot be ignored."
  • Nobody is forced to scroll. The choice is made by the user every time.
  • Content is curated by users too — your feed is shaped by what you click.
  • Digital literacy should be taught — the problem is created by lack of education.
  • Companies cannot be blamed for every choice that is made by an individual.
  • If users are educated about algorithms, they can take control of their experience.
"I think this feature was designed to…" "The data is used to…" "Users are affected by…" "I believe both the user and company…" "If this isn't regulated…"
How to run: Split class in two. Each side gets 90 seconds to present their arguments, then 30 seconds of rebuttal each. Listen for passive voice — award 1 point for each correct passive used during the debate. Announce the winner by passive count, not by "right" opinion.
Extension: After the debate, ask: "¿Cambió de opinión alguien?" This is the real B1+ skill — expressing that your opinion was changed by an argument.
§3

🎤 Relay — Active → Passive Transformation

10 min
Active sentence in → Passive sentence out. Reveal to check.
Revealed: 0 / 5
Card 1 — Present Passive
Active: "Facebook engineers collect your data every time you open the app."
→ Transform to passive. Who/what is the focus?
✅ "Your data is collected (by Facebook engineers) every time you open the app." — The agent "by engineers" can be omitted if unimportant. The data is the focus.
Card 2 — Past Passive
Active: "A large team of designers built this app in 2010."
→ Transform to past passive. Start with "This app…"
✅ "This app was built by a large team of designers in 2010." — Past passive: was + past participle. "Build" → irregular → "built".
Card 3 — Future Passive
Active: "Governments will introduce new data privacy laws next year."
→ Transform to future passive. Start with "New data privacy laws…"
✅ "New data privacy laws will be introduced (by governments) next year." — Future passive: will be + past participle. "Introduce" → regular → "introduced".
Card 4 — Passive Question
Active: "Does the algorithm show everyone the same content?"
→ Transform to passive question. Start with "Is the same content…"
✅ "Is the same content shown to everyone (by the algorithm)?" — Passive question: Is/Are + subject + past participle? "Show" → irregular → "shown".
Card 5 — Passive Negative + Context
Active: "The company doesn't protect your private information by default."
→ Transform to passive negative. Start with "Your private information…"
✅ "Your private information is not protected by default." — Passive negative: is/are + not + past participle. Natural in privacy policy language.
Relay mechanics: Student A reads the active sentence, Student B transforms it to passive without looking at the answer. Then reveal. Class votes: correct or not? Award 2 points per correct transformation.
Key teaching moment on Card 1: The "by" phrase is optional — stress this. In business English, we often drop it: "Data is collected every day" (we don't need to say by whom if it's obvious).

🎤 Solo Challenge — 2 Minutes

8 min
Two minutes. Your relationship with technology. No notes.
🎤 SOLO — Your Digital Identity (2 min)
Describe your relationship with social media and your digital identity. Tell your complete story:
Past: How you used social media before vs now Passive: Use passive voice at least 3 times Opinion: Who is responsible — you or the platform? Vocabulary: Use 3+ words from S1A (feed, algorithm, curate, identity…) Closing: One change you will make starting tomorrow
Scoring guide (assign verbally): 3 pts — correct passive structures · 2 pts — vocabulary from S1A · 2 pts — clear opinion · 1 pt — natural delivery · 1 pt — use of colloquial phrase. Max: 9 pts. Note scores for leaderboard in §7.
After each student: Ask the class: "¿Cuántos pasivos contaron?" Make counting passive voice a habit — it builds recognition naturally.

🔍 Source Analysis — Tech & Society

8 min
Read. Analyze. What passive structures do you spot?
📰 Tech News Article
"Social media platforms are used by over 5 billion people worldwide. Most users are shown content that was selected by algorithms — not by human editors. Privacy advocates warn that personal data is collected and sold without adequate user consent."
❓ According to the article, who selects the content users see?
🎙️ Podcast — Psychology Expert
"Young people are being raised in an environment where comparison is constant. Self-worth is measured in likes. If nothing is done, we will see the consequences for decades. These systems were designed by people who understood psychology — and used that knowledge commercially."
❓ What does the expert say about how these systems were designed?
📧 Government Press Release
"New data protection regulations will be introduced in 2025. Companies will be required to disclose what data is collected and how it is used. Fines of up to €20 million will be imposed if these rules are violated. Users will be given the right to request that their data be deleted."
❓ What will happen to companies that violate the new rules?
💬 Tech CEO — Interview Quote
"Our platform is used by people to share their real lives. The data that is collected is used to improve user experience — not to manipulate anyone. Every feature that is added goes through extensive testing. Users are always in control of what is shared."
❓ According to the CEO, why is data collected?
Extension task: After answering, ask: "¿Cuántas estructuras pasivas encontraron en estos 4 textos?" Count together — there are at least 12 passive structures across the 4 sources. This demonstrates how naturally passive voice appears in authentic English texts.
Discussion: "¿A quién creen más — al gobierno, al experto en psicología, o al CEO?" This is opinion language at B1+ level: "I think the expert is more reliable because…"
