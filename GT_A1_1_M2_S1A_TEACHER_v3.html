§ 01
What do you do every day?
☀️ Activates daily routine concepts · Student speaks from minute one · Zero grammar correction now
⏱ 5–8 MIN
☀️ Your morning: What time do you arrive at work? What is the first thing you do? Tell your partner — Spanish is fine for now.
💻 Look at these actions: check email · go to a meeting · eat lunch · start a report · send a message · leave the office. Which ones do you do every day? Tell your partner.
🕐 Your day in numbers: What time do you start? What time do you finish? Do you eat at your desk or outside?
⚡ Teacher note: Run this as an open conversation. 100% Spanish allowed. Write on the whiteboard the verbs the student mentions — those verbs become the §2 vocabulary. Do NOT correct grammar yet.
⚡
Quick Win — 30 seconds · You can do it!
Say 3 things you do every day at work. Start each sentence with "I…"
"I check email. I go to meetings. I eat lunch at two." — no perfect grammar, just try 🎯
Layer 1 · Pedagogical
  • ACTION: Write on the whiteboard the verbs the student mentions — those become the §2 vocab.
  • If they say "I am work every day" → note it on the whiteboard. Return to it in §4 Grammar as an error to correct.
  • Do not correct errors now. The goal: create the communicative need to talk about routines in English.
Layer 2 · Engagement
  • "You know more English than you think — now let's give it structure."
Layer 3 · Retención
  • "By the end of this session you will be able to describe your complete workday — affirmative, negative and questions. In 60 minutes."

§ 04
Simple Present — Tu Herramienta
📐 Affirmative · Negative · Yes/No Question · Short Answer · The essential structure for describing what you do every day
⏱ 12–15 MIN
🎯 Situación Real — Antes de la Regla
Your manager in the U.S. writes to you on Slack:
"Hey — describe your typical work day for me. What do you usually do?"

You need to respond in English. How do you talk about things you do every day — as a habit, as a routine?
➡ La herramienta que necesitas: Simple Present — para hábitos diarios, rutinas y hechos regulares.
↓
✅ The Tool: Simple Present (I / you / we / they)
Use it for: daily habits · routines · regular actions · general facts
Formula: Subject + base verb — for I, you, we, they, the verb does NOT change. No endings.
FormaFórmulaEjemploShort Answer
✅ AfirmativoI / You / We / They + verbo base"I check my email every morning."—
❌ NegativoI / You / We / They + don't + verbo base"I don't work on weekends."—
❓ PreguntaDo + I / you / we / they + verbo + ?"Do you start work at nine?"Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
⚠️ For Spanish speakers: In Spanish: "Trabajo todos los días" → one form. In English: "I work every day." → the verb does NOT change for I/you/we/they. Do not translate word for word.
⚡ Common Errors — Spanish Speakers
✗ "I am work every day."→✓ "I work every day."
Never use am/is/are with an action verb in Simple Present. The verb stands alone.
✗ "I don't to work on weekends."→✓ "I don't work on weekends."
After don't → base verb. No "to".
✏️ Activity 1 — Choose the correct form
Click the correct option. You will receive immediate feedback. ✅ ❌
1. "Every morning I ___ my emails before the meeting starts."
2. "I ___ work on Saturdays. I only work Monday to Friday."
3. "___ you send reports every week? — Yes, I do."
4. "We ___ lunch together every Friday. It's a good team tradition."
🔀 Activity 2 — Put the words in order
Arrastra los bloques para build the correct sentence. Click "Check" when you're done.

  • ⠿morning.

🗣️
Micro-Speaking · 90 segundos
Dile a your partner 2 cosas que haces cada día (afirmativo) + 1 que NO haces (negativo). 👥
➡ "I start work at ___. I check ___ every day. I don't work on ___."
Layer 1 · Pedagogical
  • ACTION: Return to the errors from §1 and correct them now with the table. "You said 'I am work' — look here: what is the correct form?"
  • Oral drill after the table: 6 rounds, 30 sec. "Do you start at eight?" → "Yes, I do." / "No, I don't."
  • Analogía short answer: "En español: '¿Trabajas hoy? — Sí.' En inglés: 'Yes, I do.' Mismo principio."
Layer 2 · Engagement
  • "En 10 minutos ya puedes hacer afirmaciones, negaciones Y preguntas. That is el 80% del Simple Present." ✅
Layer 3 · Retención
  • "En S2A aprenderás la 3rd person — lo que ELLA o ÉL hace. Hoy dominas el YO."
§ 05
Ana's Work Day
📖 Read Ana García's Slack message · Find Simple Present verbs in real context · Workplace reading comprehension
⏱ 10–12 MIN
👩‍💼 Ana García
Production Coordinator · Celaya, Guanajuato 🇲🇽
💬 Slack · #team-general
Monday, 8:05 AM

Hi team! 👋 I usually start work at 8 AM. First, I check my email and Slack messages. I have a quick team meeting every Monday at 9. We talk about the week's priorities.

After the meeting, I go to my desk and send the daily production report to the manager. I eat lunch at 2 PM — I don't eat at my desk. 😄

I leave the office at 6 PM. I don't work on weekends. I'm not a morning person, but I arrive on time every day! ☀️

🔵 Verbs in context: start · check · have · go · send · eat · leave · work · arrive · don't eat · don't work
✏️ Comprensión — True or False
Click en TRUE ✅ o FALSE ❌ para cada afirmación.
📍 Ana starts work at 9 AM.
📧 Ana checks her email every morning.
🍽️ Ana eats lunch at her desk every day.
📅 Ana works on weekends.
👥 Ana has a team meeting every Monday at 9.
✏️ Comprensión — Multiple Choice
5. 📋 What does Ana send to the manager after the meeting?
6. 🤔 What does "I'm not a morning person" mean?
🗣️ Now You — Production Trigger
➡ In ONE sentence, say something about your routine that is the same as Ana's AND something different.
"I also check email every morning, but I don't leave at six — I leave at seven."
⚠️ Don't write. Say the sentence out loud right now. Your partner listens. 🗣️
Layer 1 · Pedagogical
  • ACTION: Scanning technique — student underlines ALL bold verbs FIRST before reading completely.
  • Trampa intencional: "Ana starts work at 9 AM" → FALSO (son las 8). Error de lectura rápida muy común.
  • Do not advance to §6 without the student having said their oral Production Trigger. Minimum: 1 complete correct sentence.
Layer 2 · Engagement
  • "Acabas de leer un Slack message real de una colega de Celaya. Ese es el inglés exacto de tu trabajo." 🏭
Layer 3 · Retención
  • "In S1B you will listen to Ana on audio and describe YOUR routine out loud, without notes or aids."

Routine Prompt Generator
🎲 WOW component · Generate a random verb · Student produces a complete sentence · 6 rounds without repetition · Free speaking
⏱ 10–12 MIN
🌅 Question 1 — Tu mañana
What time do you arrive at work? What is the first thing you do? Describe your first 30 minutes at the office.
💡 "I arrive at ___ . First, I ___ . Then I ___."
💼 Question 2 — Tu día típico
Tell your partner 4 things you do every day at work. Use 4 different verbs from today's list.
💡 "I check… / I send… / I have… / I go… / I eat… / I work on…"
❌ Question 3 — Lo que NO haces
Tell your partner 2 things you DON'T do at work. Be specific — when don't you do them? Why?
💡 "I don't work on ___ . / I don't eat ___ . / I don't check ___ at night."
Sample Answer — Mi rutina laboral:
"I usually arrive at the office at eight thirty. First, I log in and check my email — I have about twenty messages every morning. At nine, I go to the team meeting. We discuss the day's priorities. After the meeting, I send reports to my manager. I don't eat at my desk — I go to the lunch room at two. I leave at six. I don't work on weekends."
