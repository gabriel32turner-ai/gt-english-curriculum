§ 01
Warm-Up — S2A Recall
5–7 min
⚡ Fast recall — no notes · out loud
In S2A you learned to read full dates in English and ask about distances and times. Today you use them without a safety net. Say out loud:
🗓 "April 21st" — say the full ordinal
🚕 "How long does it take from the airport to your office?"
📍 "It's about 15 km from downtown — not far at all."
📅 "The deadline is on June 30th. Please submit by 5 PM."
🎯 Quick fire — teacher asks · student answers without pausing
"What's May 15th in full English?" → May fifteenth
"How do you say 12th?" → twelfth
"By taxi or in taxi?" → by taxi
"What's 31st?" → thirty-first
⚡ Quick Win — 20 seconds
20 seconds! Say 3 events from your agenda this week: "On Monday I have a meeting. On Wednesday I have a call. The deadline is on Friday."
Layer 1 · Pedagogical
  • ACTION: Quick fire with no prep — student answers in under 3 seconds. If they hesitate, note the item and return to it at the end.
  • S2B has no new grammar. All the energy goes to output. Make it clear from the start: "Today you speak. I listen."
  • If the student struggles with "twelfth" or "fifth" — don't correct now. Work on it in §5 Games (Word Scramble).
Layer 2 · Engagement
  • "In S2A you understood the system. In S2B you prove it. That's the difference between knowing and doing."

🗣 Shadowing — 4 key sentences · Exact rhythm · Repeat each 3 times
1
"How long does it take from the airport to your office?"
2
"It takes approximately 20 minutes by taxi."
3
"The deadline is on April 21st — please submit by 5 PM."
4
"It's not far at all — walking distance from the hotel."
Layer 1 · Pedagogical
  • ACTION: Audio → student repeats → teacher gives rhythm/stress feedback. Not phoneme-by-phoneme — full-sentence rhythm.
  • Shadowing: student does NOT read while listening the first time. Listen → then read + repeat. Second time: listen and speak simultaneously.
  • "approximately" — 5 syllables: a-PROX-i-mate-ly. Stress on the 2nd. Drill it in isolation if needed.

§ 03
Listening — My Weekly Agenda (Monologue)
10–12 min
🎧
Ana García — Weekly Agenda Update (Voice Note to the Team)
ElevenLabs · Rachel (Ana) · Speed: 0.82 · ~30 sec · Monologue
▶ Show ElevenLabs Script

Ana (monologue): "Hi team, this is Ana with a quick update on my agenda for this week. On Monday I have the Q2 planning meeting at 9 AM. On Wednesday David Park arrives — it takes approximately 20 minutes from the airport by taxi, and the hotel is walking distance from our office, about 5 minutes on foot. Our Q2 review is now on May 15th. The deadline to confirm attendance is May 8th — please send me your reports before 5 PM. Thank you."

⚙️ ElevenLabs: Rachel (Ana) · eleven_multilingual_v2 · speed 0.82 · stability 0.5 · similarity 0.75 · monologue ~30 sec
Activity — Listen & Answer
Listen to the audio and answer the questions out loud — in full sentences.
1. What day does Ana have the Q2 planning meeting?
2. How long does it take from the airport to the office?
3. When is the deadline to confirm attendance?
True or False?
The hotel is walking distance from Ana's office.
Ana asks the team to send reports after 5 PM on the deadline day.
The Q2 review is now on May 15th.
Layer 1 · Pedagogical
  • ACTION: 1st listen for gist. 2nd listen to answer questions. Answers out loud — full sentences, not just the option.
  • T/F trap: Ana says "send me your reports BEFORE 5 PM" — not after. Students must catch the preposition.
  • After the questions: "Now you record the same voice note about YOUR agenda this week." Free retelling, no script.
Layer 2 · Engagement
  • "You just understood a real corporate voice note in English — no subtitles, no translation. That's A1.2 in action."
Layer 3 · Retention & Upsell
  • "In M3 you record voice notes like this for your real team — agenda, deadlines, logistics. All in English."
§ 04
Role Play — Logistics Coordinator
12–15 min
🎬 Scenario — International Client Arrival · Celaya Office
Sarah Chen (Operations Manager, Chicago) arrives in Celaya on June 3rd at 3 PM. You are the coordinator of her visit. She needs to know everything: how long it takes from the airport, how far the hotel is, when the meeting is, what the report deadline is. She doesn't have the email — only the phone, and she calls you.
🧑‍💼 STUDENT — Coordinator
Answer all of Sarah's questions. Use dates with ordinals, "It takes", "How far", "by taxi / on foot". At least 6 sentences total.
👩‍💼 TEACHER — Sarah Chen
Ask the listed questions. If the student makes a structural error, don't correct in the moment. Note it and review at the end.
✅ Must use — Student must include:
  • 2 full ordinal dates — "June third", "June thirtieth"
  • "It takes approximately…" with a means of transport
  • "How far is it…" or an answer with distance
  • "Please…" + imperative — at least 1 instruction
  • "The deadline is on…" — with an exact date
📋 Sarah's script (teacher) — Questions in order
1. "Hi! I land at Celaya airport on June 3rd at 3 PM. How long does it take to get to the office?"
2. "And my hotel — how far is it from your office? Is it walking distance?"
3. "When is our meeting? What's the exact date?"
4. "And when is the Q2 report deadline? Do I need to submit something?"
5. "Perfect. Can you send me a reminder before the meeting?" (push for imperative)
⏱ Role Play Timer — Target: 4 min
4:00

"Hi Sarah. I'm sending you my agenda for next week. I have a team meeting on Monday at 9 AM and a conference call with London on Tuesday. The training session is on Wednesday afternoon. The deadline for the report is on March 28th — please send me your input before Friday."

🗣️ 3 Phrases for This Week
Three model phrases for talking about your agenda in English. Use them in your emails and meetings. 💬
1
"I have a meeting on Monday at 10 AM."
Tengo una reunión el lunes a las 10 AM.
✅ Time prepositions: ON + day, AT + time. The most important M1 pattern.
2
"The deadline is on March 20th."
La fecha límite es el 20 de marzo.
💼 ON + full date + ordinal. For deadlines, contracts, deliveries.
3
"The conference is in June."
La conferencia es en junio.
🏆 IN + month. For broad events. Perfect contrast with ON + day.
Layer 1 · Pedagogical
  • ACTION: Role play without interruption. Note errors. At the end: 2 min of feedback — max 3 corrections, the most important.
  • If the student says "in taxi" — don't stop the role play. Note it and correct after: "Remember the rule? By taxi, by car, on foot."
  • Second round: swap. Student is Sarah, teacher is the coordinator. Harder — student must generate the questions.
  • If time allows: third round with the student's real data — their company, their address, their real deadlines.
Layer 2 · Engagement
  • "You just hosted an international client in English — with dates, logistics, and deadlines. That's exactly what you'd do at your company."

Speaking Activity 1
My Real Work Agenda — 3 min
No notes. The student presents their real agenda for next week: events, exact dates, instructions for the team. Teacher does not interrupt — only listens and takes notes.
Must include: 3 dates with full ordinals · 1 "How long" or "How far" sentence · 2 imperatives ("Please…")

Speaking Activity 2 — Debate
Work-Life Balance: Too Many Meetings?
Teacher opens the debate: "Do you have too many meetings at work?" Student responds with their opinion — at least 5 sentences. Teacher takes the opposite position to force more oral production.
Starter phrases: "I think…" · "In my company…" · "Every week we have…" · "The meeting on [date] is…"
Push questions: "How long are your meetings?" · "How far do you travel for them?" · "When was the last one?"
3:00
Layer 1 · Pedagogical
  • ACTION: Agenda Spinner — 3 rounds minimum. Real Agenda — no interruption. Debate — teacher takes the opposite stance to drive more output.
  • Agenda Spinner: if the student can't answer in 30 seconds, that's a real gap. Note the type (date / distance / instruction) and drill it more.
  • Debate: "too many meetings" is a universal topic in Mexican corporate settings. The student has a real opinion — they just need the English to express it.
  • No corrections during free speaking. Max 3 corrections at the end — the highest-impact ones for their communication.
Layer 2 · Engagement
  • "You just spoke for 3 continuous minutes in English about your real work. No script. That's not someone who 'doesn't know English.'"
Layer 3 · Retention & Upsell
  • "In M3 you present your whole company in English — agenda, structure, logistics. Today you proved you can already do it with dates and distances."
