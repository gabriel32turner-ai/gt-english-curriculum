⚡ Hot Take  ·  5 min

Mental Health at Work — Where Do You Stand?

“If companies genuinely cared about mental health, they would reduce workloads — not add wellness apps.”
You voted. Now justify your position in 30 seconds to your partner — use Second Conditional.
The Hot Take is always timed at 5 minutes max. After voting, each learner must justify to a partner using Second Conditional: “If companies really cared, they would…” This forces immediate grammar activation before the session content begins. Strong disagreement = productive tension = more language. Welcome it.
🗣 Pitch Round  ·  10 min

Argue the Position

Each pair gets one role card. You have 2 minutes to prepare, then 2 minutes to pitch. The group votes on the most convincing argument.

ROLE A — HR MANAGER
You believe that if companies invested in structured mental health support (therapy access, flexible hours, manageable workloads), employee retention would improve by 30% or more. Build the business case.
Must use: burnout, wellbeing, engagement, Second Conditional
ROLE B — CFO / FINANCE
You believe mental health is a personal responsibility. If the company paid for therapy and reduced workloads, productivity would drop and costs would rise. Make the financial counter-argument.
Must use: performance, incentive, loyalty, Second Conditional
ROLE C — EMPLOYEE
You’ve been struggling with burnout for 6 months. If your company had better support, you would not be looking for a new job right now. Share your experience and what you need.
Must use: stigma, boundary, acknowledge, Second Conditional
ROLE D — EXTERNAL COACH
You coach executives across the Bajío region. If managers were trained in emotional intelligence, team mental health metrics would improve significantly. Present your evidence.
Must use: resilience, normalize, thrive, Second Conditional
PREP TIME
PITCH TIME
Run 2-4 pitches depending on group size — not every role needs to run every session. After each pitch: 30 seconds of group feedback — vocabulary used? Second Conditional present? Argument logical? Vote by show of hands for most convincing. Keep energy high. Do not stop to correct mid-pitch.

The Relay is the creative fluency anchor of every B session. Each session has a unique visual / story format. If someone freezes, give them a vocabulary card prompt: “Use ‘boundary’ in your sentence.” Note: the story starter uses past perfect + Third Conditional exposure — correction-optional at B1.2.
⚡ Speed Round  ·  6 min

30 Seconds Per Card

Each learner picks a card. 30 seconds on the timer. Click the card when done.

Define “burnout” without using the words tired, stress, or work.
Try: exhaustion, system, breaking point…
If your company offered 1 mental health day per month, would you use it? Why or why not?
Use Second Conditional + reasoning
Give 3 examples of “setting a boundary” at work — one easy, one medium, one hard.
E.g. not checking emails after 8pm…
If the stigma around mental health disappeared tomorrow, what would change in your team?
Use would/could/might + vocabulary
Someone says: “Mental health issues are an excuse for laziness.” Respond in 30 seconds.
Use: acknowledge, normalize, thrive
What’s the difference between resilience and just putting up with bad conditions? Make a case.
Try: “Resilience is… but if the conditions…”
30s SPEED TIMER
Speed Round is high-energy, low-stakes. Learners self-select cards. If they finish under 30 seconds, reward it — don’t fill the silence. If they run over, let them finish the thought. Click the card to mark it done. Note: cards 4, 5, and 6 are harder — assign to stronger speakers. Cards 1 and 3 are accessible for lower-confidence learners.
🌟 Solo Challenge  ·  8 min

The 90-Second Argument

Your Brief
You have been asked to speak for 90 seconds at your company’s next leadership meeting. Your topic: “Why investing in employee mental health is a business decision, not a welfare decision.”

You must convince a room of directors — people who care about numbers, productivity, and retention. Use everything from S1A: vocabulary, grammar, and the colloquial phrases.
✅ Must use Second Conditional at least twice
✅ Must use at least 4 words from S1A vocabulary
✅ Must include at least one number or statistic (real or hypothetical)
✅ Must close with a clear recommendation
❌ No reading from notes — speaking only
90s SOLO TIMER
Prep 60 seconds mentally. Then speak. Peers listen and count: vocabulary words used, Second Conditionals used. Share the count after.
This is the B-session production peak. Every learner does this individually — no pairs. While one speaks, others count silently (vocabulary + grammar) and note the closing recommendation. After: 1-minute peer feedback round. Then teacher closes with 2 language points to highlight — choose from what you heard, not what you prepared. This makes feedback feel authentic and observed.

🎯 3 Phrases From This Session — Use Them This Week:
“If we normalized this conversation…”
Use it to open a sensitive topic at work without being dramatic. Signals emotional intelligence.
“I need to set a boundary on that.”
Use it professionally when declining extra work. Firm but not aggressive. Works in English meetings.
“At the end of the day, it’s a wellbeing issue.”
Reframes business decisions as human ones. Useful in any HR, management, or team conversation.
