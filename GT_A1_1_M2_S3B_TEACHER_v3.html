§ 01
Picture Prompt — How often?
🖼️ Choose a photo · Describe la escena usando adverbios de frecuencia + at/in/on · 4–5 sentences · Habla desde el primer segundo
⏱ 5–7 MIN
🚫 S3B — SIN gramática nueva · SIN lecturas · 100% producción oral · Frecuencia + at/in/on de S3A reciclados libremente
⚡
Quick Win — 30 seconds · You can do it!
Sin mirar notas: say 3 sentences sobre your routine usando 3 adverbios de frecuencia diferentes.
"I always check email. I usually have a meeting on Mondays. I never work on weekends." 🎯
👀 Choose a photo. Look at the people. Say 4–5 sentences: How often do they do this? When exactly? Use frequency adverbs (always/usually/often/sometimes/rarely/never) + at/in/on. No wrong answer!

🗓 La Junta del Lunes
ELEGIDA ✓
Photo 2
📋 El Plan Semanal
ELEGIDA ✓
Photo 3
🌙 Late at the Office?
ELEGIDA ✓
🗣️ Say 4–5 sentences sobre tu foto.
Usa: "They always ___ on ___." · "He/She usually ___ in the ___." · "They sometimes ___ at ___." · "She rarely ___." · "I never ___ on weekends."
Scaffold visible al student (si lo necesita):
"They always ___ on ___."  ·  "He/She usually ___ at ___."  ·  "They sometimes ___ in the ___."  ·  "She rarely ___."  ·  "They never ___ on weekends."
Layer 1 · Pedagogical
  • ACTION: Student must use minimum 2 different frequency adverbs + 1 time preposition (at/in/on) in their sentences.
  • Photo 3 (late at the office) is the richest — it prompts "She rarely works late" / "She sometimes stays until eight" — low-frequency adverbs that get little practice.
  • Do not correct the adverb position in the moment. Listen and take note. The position is reinforced in §3 Audio Model.
  • If the student uses "She always in the morning check email" → nota el error de posición pero no interrumpas. Lo trabajas en §3.
Layer 2 · Engagement
  • "In S3A you needed the visual scale to remember the percentages. Today you use always and rarely from memory. That IS progress." 📈
Layer 3 · Retención
  • "En 50 minutos describirás your week laboral completa día por día — con frecuencia y horarios exactos. Este picture prompt es el calentamiento." 📅

§ 03
Audio Model — Ana's Frequency Report
🎧 Ana describe sus hábitos con frecuencia + at/in/on · ElevenLabs model · Shadowing 3 frases · Luego tú describes your week igual que Ana
⏱ 8–10 MIN
🎙️ Ana García — Weekly Frequency Report (completo)
ElevenLabs · speed 0.78 · Listen completely first · Then shadowing phrase by phrase ↓

🔊 Listen to each phrase → repeat out loud → listen again. Pay attention to the adverb position and at/in/on. Minimum 3 rounds.

🔊 Shadowing — Las 3 Frases Modelo de S3A
Each phrase has the adverb in the correct position + time preposition. Say all 3 phrases without stopping — that is fluency.
1
"I always check my email in the morning."
2
"I usually attend the Monday meeting at nine."
3
"I never work on weekends."
🎙️ Now You — Mismo Formato que Ana
Describe 3 hábitos de your week laboral real — igual que Ana. Usa 3 adverbios diferentes + at/in/on al menos 2 veces. Without notes.
➡ "I always ___ in the morning. I usually ___ on ___. I never ___ on weekends."
Sample Answer — Rutina con frecuencia:
"I always arrive at the office at eight thirty — every single day. I usually check my email first and then go to my computer. I often have meetings on Monday mornings. I sometimes stay late on Fridays when there's a deadline. I never work on Sundays — that's my rule. On weekends, I always disconnect completely from work."
Layer 1 · Pedagogical
  • ACTION: Shadowing — student dice la frase ANTES que el audio. Verifica posición del adverbio y preposición correcta.
  • In the "Now You": if the student says "I check always email" → immediate recast: "I ALWAYS check email — do you hear the difference?"
  • Regresa a los errores del §1 que anotaste. Si hubo errores de posición → usa la frase 1 del shadowing como modelo de corrección.
  • El "Now You" es el precalentamiento directo para el Role Play de §4 y el My Week Calendar de §5.
Layer 2 · Engagement
  • "You just described your habits exactly the way Ana would in a welcome email to her new colleague. That is already functional work English." 💼
Layer 3 · Retención
  • "En §5 vas a llenar un calendario completo de your week — día por día, con frecuencia y horarios. El audio fue el modelo. El siguiente paso es tuyo." 📅

Role Play — Describe Your Week
🎭 Main oral activity · 3 progressive rounds · Describe your real work week with frequency + at/in/on
⏱ 12–15 MIN
🎭 Round 1 — Guided (scaffold visible)
📞 "Tell me about your typical week"
Your new colleague in Chicago wants to know about your work week. The scaffold is visible. Minimum 5 sentences.
👔 Teacher (new U.S. colleague)
Sarah Kim · Chicago
Empieza: "Hi! What does your typical week look like? How often do you have meetings? Do you ever work on weekends?"
🗣️ Student
Your name · Celaya
Describe your week real. Usa al menos 3 adverbios de frecuencia diferentes + at/in/on al menos 2 veces. 5+ sentences.
Scaffold Round 1:
"I always ___ in the morning, at ___."
"I usually have a meeting on ___ at ___."
"I sometimes ___ in the afternoon."
"I rarely ___ on ___."
"I never ___ on weekends."
🎭 Round 2 — Semi-free (teacher questions only)
📞 Second call — without scaffold
Same situation. No scaffold. Teacher guides with specific questions.
👔 Teacher — questions
Prompts disponibles
"How often do you have meetings?" · "When do you usually send reports?" · "Do you work on Fridays?" · "What time do you usually start?" · "Do you ever work late?"
🗣️ Student — free
No scaffold
Respuestas completas con adverbio + preposición. "I usually have meetings on Mondays at nine."
⭐ Round 3 — Libre (Challenge: 90 segundos)
🎤 Full Week Report
90 segundos. Sin teacher como interlocutor. Student describe their week completa usando las 6 palabras de frecuencia y AT, IN, ON al menos una vez cada una.
🏆 Objetivo: 6 adverbios + AT + IN + ON = ⭐ Excelente · 4–5 adverbios + 2 prep = ✅ Bien · menos = 📋 Practica la semana laboral oral en casa
Layer 1 · Pedagogical
  • ACTION: R1 con scaffold. R2 con preguntas. R3 cronómetro 90 seg, cuenta adverbios + preposiciones correctas.
  • En R3: no interrumpas. Escucha y marca mentalmente. Después: "Usaste 'always' 3 veces — eso es automaticidad. Usaste 'on Monday at nine' perfecto."
  • If in R2 the student says "I have a meeting in Monday" → gentle recast: "On Monday — do you hear the difference?"
Layer 2 · Engagement
  • "Acabas de describir your week laboral en inglés con horarios, días y frecuencias. That is lo que necesitas en una llamada con EE.UU. 📞"
Layer 3 · Retención
  • "En §5 llevas esto al siguiente nivel — calendario real, día por día. El role play fue el entrenamiento." 📅
§ 05
My Week Calendar
📅 Componente WOW · Your real work week · Day by day · Adverbios de frecuencia + at/in/on en contexto auténtico · 100% producción oral
⏱ 10–12 MIN
📅 My Week Calendar
Describe your week laboral real — día por día, en inglés. Without notes.
Regla: Para cada día di 2–3 sentences: (1) algo que siempre/usualmente haces ese día · (2) a qué hora o en qué momento del día · (3) algo que raramente/nunca haces ese día.
Use frequency adverbs + AT, IN, ON for each day. 5 days × 3 sentences = 15 sentences · That is fluency.

Si bloqueo MON
"Do you always have a meeting on Monday? What time? In the morning or afternoon?"
Si bloqueo FRI
"Do you work from home on Fridays? Sometimes? Rarely? Never?"
Challenge extra
"Ahora describe el horario de tu manager — usa 'he/she usually' + ON/IN/AT." (Prepara S4A: Wh- questions)
Layer 1 · Pedagogical
  • ACTION: Student describe el calendario out loud — tú tomas notas de errores de at/in/on y posición del adverbio. Feedback al final.
  • Minimum aceptable: 3 días descritos con al menos 1 adverbio + 1 preposición cada uno. That is S3B exitosa a A1.1.
  • Extra challenge: if the student reaches FRI correctly, ask them to describe their manager's schedule (3rd person). That is exactly the gap S4A covers — use it as a natural bridge.
  • If the student says "on in the morning" → recast: "IN the morning — or: on Monday morning. Which applies here?"
Layer 2 · Engagement
  • "Acabas de describir your week laboral completa en inglés — con horarios y frecuencias exactas. That is lo que escribirías en un email de bienvenida como el de Ana." 📧
Layer 3 · Retención
  • "En S4A agregas las preguntas: 'When do you usually have meetings? How often do you send reports?' Hoy tienes las respuestas perfectas. El siguiente paso es hacer las preguntas." 🔄
§ 06
Debate — Práctica Oral Libre
🗣️ 3 preguntas sobre hábitos laborales reales · Adverbios de frecuencia en contexto de opinión · Sin estructura obligatoria
⏱ 8–10 MIN
💬 Question 1 — Hábitos de email
How often do you check your work email? Do you always check it in the morning? Do you sometimes check it at night or on weekends?
➡ Usa: "I always/usually/sometimes check email ___." · "I rarely check it at night." · "I never check email on weekends — or do you?"
💬 Question 2 — La reunión ideal
How often should a team have meetings? Is once a week enough? Do meetings always start on time in your company?
➡ Usa: "In my company, we usually have meetings ___." · "They always/rarely start on time." · "I think meetings should ___ on ___ at ___."
⭐ Question 3 — Challenge
Think of the most organized person you know at work. Describe their schedule using always, usually, often, and never. At least 5 sentences.
➡ Free — frequency adverbs + at/in/on · Teacher takes note of correct at/in/on to celebrate at the end.
Layer 1 · Pedagogical
  • ACTION: Mínima corrección. Solo recast suave de errores de at/in/on o posición del adverbio. Deja que fluya la opinión.
  • Question 3 introduces 3rd person with adverbs — natural preparation for S4A. If student says "She always check" without -s → do NOT corras. That is exactamente la brecha de S4A.
  • Question 2 ("should there be many meetings?") usually generates rich Mexico-US cultural conversation. Let them wander — it is authentic oral production.
Layer 2 · Engagement
  • "Estás dando tu opinión sobre el trabajo en inglés usando 'always, usually, never'. No estás traduciendo. Estás pensando en inglés." 🧠
Layer 3 · Retención
  • "In S4A you learn to ASK: 'How often do you check email? When do you usually have meetings?' Today you have the answers. Next class: the questions." 🔑
