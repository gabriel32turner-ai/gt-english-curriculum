Colloquial Phrases — Natural Tech Talk
⏱ 8 min · 5 key phrases
"I compare myself to people online without realizing it."
Automatic social comparison – very common feeling.
"After scrolling for 30 minutes, I felt bad. I compare myself without realizing it."
"I re-read before I send."
Professional habit to avoid miscommunication.
"Important emails: I re-read before I send — especially in English."
"I'm not sure I trust it completely."
Healthy skepticism about AI or automated decisions.
"ChatGPT writes fast, but I'm not sure I trust it completely for legal documents."
"It's getting more powerful."
Describing rapid tech evolution.
"AI image generation? It's getting more powerful every month."
"Sometimes it's better just to call."
When text causes confusion, voice is better.
"This thread is a mess — sometimes it's better just to call."

Grammar — Passive Voice Mastery
⏱ 15 min · tenses + by-phrase + spectrum

Form recap: Present: is/are + p.p. | Past: was/were + p.p. | Future: will be + p.p. | Modal: should/can/must + be + p.p.
Key rule: Keep "by + agent" ONLY when it adds necessary information. Drop it when obvious/unknown.

🔬 Passive Spectrum — Click a sentence
Full active
"Facebook engineers built this feature."
Active impersonal
"Millions use this app daily."
Passive + by
"This app was designed by a team."
Pure passive
"Your data is collected every second."
