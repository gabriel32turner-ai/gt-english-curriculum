§1 · Hot Take — 8 min
Problem Opinions
React fast · Speak first · No notes
⏱ 8 min
⚡ Teacher Note — Hot Take

Remember from S3A: Ana at Solaris had broken printers, no badge access, and needed Carlos to help. Start with: "Tell me ONE thing Ana could NOT do." Students answer from memory — no notes. Then hit the Hot Take. Badge b1 fires when the class completes it.

🔁 Remember from S3A
Ana said: "There is no printer working on the second floor." — "I can't print the quality reports." — "Sorry about that!"
🔥 Hot Take — Agree or Disagree?
"When something is broken at work, it is MY problem — not IT's problem."
🔥 Statement 2
"Saying 'Sorry about that' is enough when you make a mistake at work."
🔥 Statement 3
"I feel confident reporting a problem in English to my manager."
🔥 Statement 4
"There are NO problems I can't describe in English after today."
💡 Engagement Layer

Statement 3 will generate real answers. Note who says NO — give them extra speaking time in §2 and §3. The goal of S3B is to change their answer to YES by §7.

§2 · Pitch & Debate — 12 min
Report It. Solve It. Live.
Role-play · Problem Frame · Class Discussion
⏱ 12 min
⚡ Teacher Note — Pitch Setup

The PROBLEM + LOCATION + SOLUTION frame from S3A. Students A and B role-play a call to the IT helpdesk. Run twice and swap. Push for the full frame: problem + location + can/can't + solution request. Watch for: "I can't to open" → correct immediately to "I can't open."

📞 Student A — Employee
Call IT to report: (1) The printer on your floor is broken. (2) You can't print the afternoon report. (3) Ask if someone can come and fix it before 3 PM. Use the full PROBLEM + LOCATION + SOLUTION frame.
Start: "Hi, I need to report a problem. The printer on the [floor] is broken. I can't print..."
💻 Student B — IT Helpdesk
Respond to the call. Say: (1) "Let me check." (2) Confirm you can or can't send someone. (3) Offer one alternative: "There is a printer on floor [X] you can use." Be professional and helpful.
Use: "Let me check... / There is a printer on... / I can send someone at..."
Class Discussion — 3 Rounds
T–S
Your manager asks: "Is everything OK?" Report ONE real problem from your workplace in English. Use the frame.
S–S
Partner A reports a problem. Partner B responds with "Let me check" + one solution. 90 seconds. Swap.
Class
Vote: what is the hardest part of this conversation in English? Vocabulary? Grammar? Confidence?
📚 Upsell Moment

After the class vote, say: "In A1.2, you will do this same conversation but add past tense: 'The printer broke yesterday.' You are almost there." This creates forward momentum and reduces anxiety about the transition to the next level.

§3 · Relay — 10 min
Problem Chain Relay
Build the report · Each student adds one sentence
⏱ 10 min
⚡ Teacher Note — Relay Rules

Students build a full workplace problem report together, one sentence each. The chain must be logical — if a sentence breaks the logic, the class stops and repairs it together. Award badge b2 when the chain reaches 6+ coherent sentences without stopping. Encourage use of ALL S3A structures.

Student 1"There is a problem on the second floor."
→
Student 2"The printer is broken."
→
Student 3"I can't print the reports."
→
Student 4"There is no IT technician available."
→
Student 5+Continue the story...
🎯 Relay Sentence Starters
"There is no ___"
"There are no ___"
"I can / I can't ___"
"Let me check."
"Can you ___?"
"Sorry about that!"
💡 Retention Layer

The relay works because it forces LISTENING, not just speaking. Each student must hear the previous sentence before adding their own. This replicates a real conversation dynamic at A1.1 level and builds listening comprehension incidentally. If the chain breaks, treat it as a teaching moment: "What structure can repair this sentence?"

§4 · Speed Round — 8 min
30-Second Situation Sprint
6 situations · 30 seconds each · Full frame required
⏱ 8 min
⚡ Teacher Note — Speed Round

Teacher reads the situation. Student speaks for 30 seconds using the PROBLEM + LOCATION + SOLUTION frame. Class counts the structures used. If they use all three parts of the frame AND a colloquial phrase, they score a bonus point for the leaderboard in §6.

Sprint 1
The elevator in your building is broken. You have a meeting on the 8th floor in 5 minutes. Report the problem and your solution.
Sprint 2
There are no meeting rooms available for your 2 PM presentation. Report this to your coordinator and ask for help.
Sprint 3
Your visitor's pass is not working at the entrance. The visitor has a meeting in 10 minutes. What do you say to security?
Sprint 4
Your computer screen is broken and you can't see anything. You have work to do. Report this to IT and ask for an urgent fix.
Sprint 5
There is no internet on your entire floor. Three colleagues can't work. Who do you call? What do you say?
Sprint 6 — Bonus
BONUS: Report TWO problems in one sentence. Use "and" to connect them. Include a solution for each. 30 seconds. No notes.
§5 · Solo Challenge + Match Up — 12 min
Grammar in Action
Solo timed report · Match Up Mini-Game
⏱ 12 min
⚡ Teacher Note — Solo Challenge First

Give students 30 seconds to think, then 90 seconds to speak. The task is to report a real problem from their workplace using all grammar from S3A: there is/are + can/can't + location + solution. While they speak, write 2–3 errors silently on the board. Correct collectively after the timer ends, not during. Then run Match Up as a grammar consolidation game.

⏱ Solo Challenge — 90 seconds
Report a REAL problem from your workplace. Use the full frame in English.
  • →Say WHAT the problem is
  • →Say WHERE it is (floor / area / department)
  • →Say what you can or can't do because of it
  • →Ask for a solution

§7 · Wrap-Up — 8 min
Spin & Close
Spin the Wheel · 3 Phrases · Certificate · My Win
⏱ 8 min
⚡ Teacher Note — Spin the Wheel

Run Spin the Wheel for 3–4 minutes before closing. Each spin gives a student a random problem situation. They have 20 seconds to say ONE sentence using the S3 grammar. Fast, fun, and reinforces the structures one final time before the end of class. Then close with 3 Phrases + Certificate + My Win.

🎲 Spin the Wheel — Final Round
▼
Your situation:
Spin to get a situation!
Say ONE sentence about this situation using S3A grammar. 20 seconds. No notes.
🎯 This week, you can use these 3 phrases at work:
Phrase 1
"The ___ is broken."
The most direct way to report any broken item. Works in a call, an email, or face-to-face. Replace the blank with any equipment name.
Phrase 2
"I can't ___ because ___."
Use to explain the IMPACT of the problem: "I can't print because the printer is broken." Managers need to understand why the problem matters, not just what it is.
Phrase 3
"Can you fix it before ___?"
Add a time deadline to your solution request. "Can you fix it before 3 PM?" This is professional, clear, and shows you understand urgency in English.
