What do you usually do on weekends? How often? — Speak for 30 seconds without stopping.
Activates S1: hobbies + frequency. Target: at least 3 adverbs or frequency expressions.
2
Give me 3 work instructions — imperative, negative, polite — about a call we’re about to start.
Activates S1A: imperatives. No scaffold. Produce them unaided in 20 seconds.
3
Tell me 2 upcoming dates at your company — full ordinal, month, year.
Activates S2: ordinals + prepositions. "The deadline is on April twenty-first."
4
Describe your manager’s daily routine — 3 sentences, 3rd person, 3 different adverbs.
Activates S3: routine + 3rd person + adverb in correct position.
5
Ask me 3 Wh- questions about my company — using different Wh- words each time.
Activates S4: Wh- + do/does correctly. Teacher answers in character as David Park.

Summarize your week in English: "I have a meeting on Tuesday at 10. The deadline is on Friday. I usually go to the gym after work." Full M1 combo!

Vocab Review — M1 Word Bank
8–10 min

Student says the term in their own sentence — then taps 🔊 to verify.

S1 — Free Time & Agenda
  • open / submit / confirm — imperativo → Open the file. Submit the report.
  • Don’t + verb — negativo → Don’t share the link.
  • twice a week / once a month → I work out twice a week.
  • on + fecha / in + mes → on April 21st / in June
  • free time / hobby → I usually read. I play sports.
S2 — Corporate Agenda
  • proposal / agenda / reminder → Please set a reminder for the 15th.
  • 1st–31st — ordinales → April 7th / June 3rd
  • It takes / It’s about → It takes 20 min by taxi.
  • postpone / quarter / headquarters → Q2 kickoff is on April 7th.
  • by taxi / on foot → by car / by bus / on foot
S3 — Work Routine
  • check emails / log in / wrap up → I always check emails first thing.
  • commute / review / follow up → She commutes 45 min by car.
  • skip lunch / swamped / work from home → I sometimes skip lunch when swamped.
  • always / usually / rarely / never → She always reviews reports.
  • I’m not a morning person / swamped → GT colloquial
S4 — Wh- Questions
  • What time does…? → What time does your meeting start?
  • How long does it take/last? → How long does your commute take?
  • How often do/does…? → How often does the team meet?
  • How many [noun] do you…? → How many direct reports do you have?
  • How far is it from…? → Con “is” — no do/does
Layer 1 · Pedagogical
  • ACTION: Student reads the term → says it in their own sentence → then taps 🔊 to verify. Not the other way around.
  • Don’t read all 20 terms. Focus on blocks where the student struggled in the Warm-Up.
  • Target time: 6–8 min. Quick pace. This section is activation, not new teaching.
Layer 2 · Engagement
  • "Look — four blocks, twenty terms, four sessions. Everything you learned in M1 is on this page."
Layer 3 · Retention & Upsell
  • "In M2 you add 20 more terms — company, products, quantities. Built on this same base."
§ 03
Grammar Flash — M1 Rules at a Glance
8–10 min

Summary of the four module grammar points. Student says the rule from memory before seeing the example.

S1 — Imperative
Aff: Verb (base) → Open / Submit / Confirm
Neg: Don’t + verb → Don’t share / Don’t miss
Polite: Please + verb → Please send the report
⚠️ Never: "Please you open" / "Don’t to share"
S2 — Dates & Distance
Exact date: on + ordinal → on April 7th
Month/year: in + month/year → in June / in 2025
Distance: It’s about ___ km
Time: It takes ___ by taxi
⚠️ "in Monday" → on Monday / "in taxi" → by taxi
S3 — Frequency Adverbs
Position: S + ADV + verb → I always check
With to be: to be + ADV → She is always on time
3rd person: She always reviews
With doesn’t: verb base → doesn’t usually skip
⚠️ "I check always" ✗ → "I always check" ✓
S4 — Wh- Questions
Structure: Wh- + do/does + S + verb
"What time does it start?" / "How often do you…?"
Exception: How far is it (to be, no do/does)
After does: verb base (no -s)
⚠️ "How often your team has" ✗ → "does your team have" ✓

4 cards — the most frequent error from each session. Say the correction before flipping.

⚠️ S1 Error
✗ "Please you open"
✓ "Please open the file."
El imperativo NUNCA tiene sujeto explícito.
⚠️ S2 Error
✗ "in Monday" / "in taxi"
✓ "on Monday" / "by taxi"
Días → on. Transporte → by. Excepción: on foot.
⚠️ S3 Error
✗ "She always review"
✓ "She always reviews"
3ª persona aff. = siempre +s. Con doesn’t → base form.
⚠️ S4 Error
✗ "How often your team has"
✓ "How often does your team have?"
Wh- + does + S + verb base. Does carga la 3ª persona.
Layer 1 · Pedagogical
  • ACTION: Read the error on the card → student gives the correction BEFORE clicking.
  • If the student fails on one grammar point → spend 2 extra min there before §4. Adjust §4 timing.
Layer 2 · Engagement
  • "Four rules. Four errors. Four corrections. That’s all of M1. You’ve got them."
Layer 3 · Retention & Upsell
  • "The S3 error — '3rd person +s' — you reuse in M4 when describing a colleague’s symptoms: 'She always arrives early but today she has a fever.' Same pattern."

      § 05
      Speaking Rounds — Pre-Exit Warm-Up
      12–15 min

      3 progressive speaking rounds. Each round prepares the next. The Exit Task is 15 minutes away.

      Round 1 — Individual Components
      Format: Rapid Fire · 30 sec per prompt · Don’t overthink

      "Say: your 2 hobbies + frequency · 1 work date (ordinal) · your manager’s morning routine (3rd person) · 1 Wh- question about my company."

      4 isolated prompts — each activates a different session. Student answers in sequence without pausing. If they freeze → "Next!" and move on.

      hobby + frequency date ordinal 3rd person routine Wh- question
      Round 2 — Connected Paragraph
      Format: 60 seconds · Connected · Real data

      "Describe your work week: when you start, how often you have meetings, your boss’s routine, and your next deadline. Use connectors: and / but / because / also."

      Student speaks for 60 seconds without stopping. Don’t correct mid-turn. Note 1 error max to address at the end of the round.

      Timer
      1:00
      Round 3 — Integrated Conversation
      Format: Role play · 90 seconds · Teacher as David Park

      "You are YOU. David Park calls for small talk before a meeting. He asks Wh- questions about your company. You answer AND ask your own questions about Phoenix. Close with one instruction and one date."

      Teacher asks 3 unexpected questions as David. Student answers and generates their own questions. This is the Exit Task in practice form — same format, 30 seconds shorter. Suggested David questions: "What time does your Monday meeting start?" / "How often do you travel?" / "How many people are on your team?"

      Timer
      1:30
      Layer 1 · Pedagogical
      • ACTION: The 3 rounds are an intentional escalation: components → paragraph → conversation. Don’t skip to Round 3 without doing the prior ones.
      • Round 2: if the student stops before 30 sec → 2-word prompt: "And your manager?" / "What about the deadline?"
      • Round 3 is the Exit Task rehearsal. If it flows well → the student reaches §6 with high confidence.
      Layer 2 · Engagement
      • "That Round 3 you just did — that’s almost exactly the Exit Task. The only difference is 30 extra seconds. You’ve got this."
      Layer 3 · Retention & Upsell
      • "In M2 the integration speaking is 4 minutes and adds company description. This 3-minute one is the foundation."
      § 06
      🎯 Exit Task — M1 Final Speaking
      15–20 min
      Exit Task · Module 1 · A1.2 · Speaking 3 minutes
      Present Your Work Week to David Park
      You are a Mexican professional talking with David Park, Sales Director from Phoenix, Arizona. You have 3 minutes to show everything you learned in M1 — no notes, no template, no help. The teacher listens and evaluates.
      🗣 Small Talk (S1)
      Opens with a hobby + frequency. Gives 1 instruction using the imperative to the teacher before the meeting starts. Natural, fluid.
      📅 Agenda & Dates (S2)
      Mentions at least 2 dates with full correct ordinal. Uses "on" with exact dates, "in" with months. Mentions distance or time if relevant.
      ⏰️ Work Routine (S3)
      Describes own routine with correctly positioned adverb + describes a colleague’s routine in 3rd person with correct -s.
      ❔ Wh- Questions (S4)
      Asks at least 3 Wh- questions to David about his company: What time, How often, How long — with correct do/does and base verb.
      Scaffold (visible to the student during the Exit Task)
      Opener: "Good morning David! I ___ on weekends — I ___ [frequency]. Great to talk to you."
      Instruction: "Before we start — please ___ / don’t ___."
      My agenda: "This week I have ___ on [ordinal]. The deadline for ___ is on ___."
      My routine: "I usually ___ at ___. I [adv] ___. My [colleague] always ___s ___."
      Questions for David: "What time does your team ___? How often do you ___? How many ___ do you have?"
      Close: "Please ___ before [date]. I look forward to ___."
      Exit Task Timer
      3:00
      • Opened with hobby + frequency small talk — natural, no long pauses.
      • Gave at least 1 instruction with a correct imperative.
      • Mentioned 2 dates with full ordinal and correct preposition (on/in).
      • Described OWN routine with adverb in the right position.
      • Described a colleague’s routine with 3rd person and correct -s.
      • Asked 3 Wh- questions to David with correct do/does and base verb.
      • Completed the full 3 minutes without stopping for more than 5 seconds at a time.
      Mastery A1.2 M1: 5 out of 7 checklist criteria met + continuous 3-min speaking = full module mastery. The student is ready for M2.

      "OK, my agenda for next week: I have a conference call with London on Monday at 9 AM. The team meeting is on Wednesday afternoon. The deadline for the quarterly report is on March 28th. For my colleague: please review the proposal before our call. Don't forget to send your comments by Friday. By the way, how often do you travel for work? I usually travel about once a month."

      🗣️ 3 Module 1 Phrases
      The three most important phrases from Module 1. Use them at work this week. 💬
      1
      "I have a meeting on Monday at 10 AM."
      Tengo una reunión el lunes a las 10 AM.
      ✅ ON + day + AT + time. The golden formula of M1.
      2
      "Please send me the report by Friday."
      Por favor envíame el informe para el viernes.
      💼 Imperative with please. Professional tone for any email.
      3
      "How often do you travel for work?"
      ¿Con qué frecuencia viajas por trabajo?
      🏆 How often + simple present. For small talk in international meetings.
      Layer 1 · Pedagogical
      • ACTION: Present the Exit Task with ceremony. "This is the moment of the module. 3 minutes. Everything you learned. Ready?"
      • During the 3 minutes: do NOT correct. Do NOT interrupt. DO answer as David Park when the student asks Wh- questions.
      • If the student freezes before 60 sec → say: "And David asks: 'What time does your meeting start?'" — reactivate without breaking flow.
      • Review the checklist WITH the student, not silently. "Look — we checked 6 out of 7. Which was the hardest?"
      • Mastery: 5/7 + 3 continuous min = ready for M2. Even 4/7 with visible effort is a success to celebrate.
      Layer 2 · Engagement
      • "You just spoke 3 minutes of corporate English with no notes, no script, no translation. That’s what your company needs. That’s M1."
      • Celebrate specifically: "Your hobby was perfect." / "Your Wh- questions — did you hear yourself say 'does'? Perfect."
      Layer 3 · Retention & Upsell
      • "In M2 you add: 'What does your company do? How many employees? What products?' — same format, new vocabulary. The M2 Exit Task is 4 minutes long."
      • "Did you know this can be recorded as evidence of your level for HR? Many companies use it as a corporate English benchmark."
