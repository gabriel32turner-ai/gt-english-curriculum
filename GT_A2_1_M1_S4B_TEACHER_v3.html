Warm-Up — Negative Speed Round
5 min

No models. The teacher gives the context. The student produces the negative sentence + duration expression INSTANTLY. Click the card once you've answered. ⚡

S1
You worked on a report but couldn't finish it.
"I didn't ___ it. I worked on it ___."
✓
S2
Your team had a 3-hour meeting but made no decision.
"We didn't ___. The meeting lasted ___."
✓
S3
A client emailed you but you had meetings all day.
"I didn't ___. I was in meetings ___."
✓
S4
The supplier never sent the documents you needed.
"We didn't ___. We waited ___."
✓
S5
You tried to resolve a complaint but worked until late.
"I didn't ___. I worked on it ___."
✓
S6
BONUS — Use an IRREGULAR negative: didn't go / didn't have / didn't make.
"I didn't ___ [irregular] because I ___."
✓
Quick check: Does the student say "I didn't finished"? → Immediate correction: didn't + BASE always. "During three hours"? → It's only for.
Layer 1 · Pedagogical
  • S6 is the key diagnostic: does the student spontaneously produce a negative with an irregular (didn't go, didn't have, didn't make)? Yes → successful S3+S4 consolidation. No → 3 min of extra drill before role play.
  • Demand REAL data. S2: "Your team" → the student's actual team. Was there a meeting like this this week?
Layer 2 · Engagement
  • "6 situations, 6 real sentences from your work. No notes. That's exactly what you'd tell your director tomorrow in English."
Layer 3 · Growth & Retention
  • "S5 puts it all together: regular verbs + irregulars + negatives + Wh- questions + duration. Today is the last drilling before the Exit Task."

⚡ Oral Duration Expression Drill — 6 rounds
The teacher says a duration: "for 3 hours" → the student forms a REAL negative sentence about their work using that expression. No repeating the same activity twice. Minimum 6 rounds with all 6 expressions: for / from…to / all morning / all afternoon / until / most of the day.
Layer 1 · Pedagogical
  • Flashcard 6 (irregular) is the most important: "I didn't go to the meeting." If the student says "I didn't went" → immediate correction, the student repeats it correctly before continuing.
  • Duration Drill: if the student uses the same expression twice → "Try a different one this time." The goal is to actively activate all 6 expressions.

Listening — The Extended Standup

🎧
Monday Standup Monologue — David's Week Recap

▶ Show ElevenLabs Script

Voice: Adam (single speaker, employee giving a Monday recap to the team) · Rate 0.82 · Stability 0.65 · ~95 sec


Good morning, team. Quick update from me on last week — I want to be transparent because several tasks are still open on the tracker.


First, I didn't finish the client proposal. I worked on it from Monday to Thursday, about twelve hours total — most of the week, honestly. The blocker was that I didn't receive the final budget numbers from finance, so I didn't achieve the quality I wanted. I'll complete it today and send it before noon.


Second, I didn't go to the supplier meeting on Wednesday. I had a crisis with the IT system all morning — from nine to noon — and I couldn't leave the office. I notified the supplier and we rescheduled for Thursday next week.


Third, I didn't reply to three client emails. I worked until seven PM on Thursday and simply didn't have the time. I'll respond to all of them before ten this morning.


That's the honest summary. My priority today is the proposal — I'll spend three hours on it this morning and submit it before lunch. Thanks for your patience.

ElevenLabs: Adam (male voice, single speaker monologue). Rate 0.82, stability 0.65. Upload to Google Drive with iframe /preview.
"Count every 'didn't' in the audio. How many total?" (Target: 7 — didn't finish, didn't receive, didn't achieve, didn't go, didn't leave/couldn't, didn't reply, didn't have)
First listen: just count.
"Write down every duration expression you hear." (from Monday to Thursday / most of the week / all morning / from nine to noon / until seven PM)
Second listen: focus only on duration expressions.
"How did David explain each thing he didn't do? Was his explanation professional?" Use the model: 'I didn't X because Y. I worked on it for Z.'
Oral evaluation — the student reconstructs David's 3 explanations.
"Now YOU give the same kind of standup. Imagine the team asks: 'What happened last week?' — Give YOUR real version. Use 3 negatives + 3 different duration expressions. No notes."
Minimum 4 negative sentences. At least 1 irregular (didn't go / didn't have / didn't make).
Layer 1 · Pedagogical
  • Task 4 is the main diagnostic before the role play. Can the student produce 3 negatives + 3 duration expressions spontaneously about their real work? Yes → ready for the full RP. No → 3 min of extra drill.
  • David uses "didn't go" (irregular negative) — point this out to the student: "Notice David doesn't say 'didn't went'? BASE always."
Layer 2 · Engagement
  • "David closes with 'Thanks for your patience.' In professional English, honest detail is more valued than dodging the problem. David does it well — now you."
Layer 3 · Growth & Retention
  • "David says at the end: 'I'll spend three hours on it this morning.' Notice how he gives a specific amount of time. That's what separates a vague update from a professional one."

10–12 min
🎮 Wordwall — External Game
Duration + Negative Sentence Match

Pairs: "3-hour meeting" ↔ "for three hours" / "9 AM to noon" ↔ "from 9 to noon" / "whole afternoon" ↔ "all afternoon" / "finished at 8 PM" ↔ "until 8 PM" / "most of the time" ↔ "most of the day" / "from Monday to Wednesday" ↔ "from Monday to Wednesday"

Instruction: "Match the situation with the correct duration. Watch out for the wrong ones!"
🎯 GT Oral Game — Friday Update Challenge
The teacher gives the context. The student produces the complete correct sentence in under 8 seconds.
Round 1 — Duration selection
Situation
"The meeting started at 2 PM and ended at 5 PM." — Which expression do you use?
Round 2 — Negative + duration sentence
Situation
"You were in meetings the whole morning. You couldn't reply to the client." — Form the complete professional sentence.
Round 3 — Irregular negative + duration
Situation
"You needed to go to the supplier meeting but stayed at the office working until 7 PM." — Express this correctly.
Round 4 — Your real week
The student gives 3 real sentences from their week — each with: negative + a different duration expression + a brief reason. The teacher asks one Wh- question after each: "Why didn't you? / How long did it take? / What did you do instead?"
Layer 1 · Pedagogical
  • Round 2: option 1 has "didn't replied" + "all the morning" — two simultaneous errors. If the student picks it: teach both errors in sequence, not at once.
  • Round 3: option 1 has "didn't went" + "until to" — the two most common S3+S4 errors together. Reinforce: BASE after didn't, "until" without "to".
  • Round 4: the teacher's Wh- questions activate S3 + S4 simultaneously — "Why didn't you?" connects negative+reason; "How long did it take?" activates duration.
Layer 2 · Engagement
  • "Round 4 with real data: that's exactly your Monday standup in English. No notes."
Layer 3 · Growth & Retention
  • "S5 is the Exit Task — you use all of M1 in a 3-min report. Negatives + duration are half the content. Today you have them automated."

🎭 Role Play 1 — Friday Update (You + Manager)
It's Friday at 5 PM. Your manager (the teacher) calls you for the weekly update. You must honestly report what you DIDN'T complete — with duration and reason — plus what you DID accomplish. Format: same as Valeria Torres' email from S4A.
You (Student — Employee)
Report: minimum 3 things you didn't do + duration for each. At least 1 irregular negative (didn't go / didn't have / didn't make). Close with next steps: "This week I will…"
Manager (Teacher)
Ask 5 Wh- questions after each negative: "Why didn't you? / How long? / What did you do instead? / When will you finish? / Who did you mention this to?"
[Manager]: "It's Friday. Before we close the week — what happened? I see several tasks still pending."
Goal: the student produces 3+ negative sentences with correct duration (for/from-to/all/until), answers Wh- questions about their negatives, and gives concrete next steps. Evaluate: is "didn't + BASE" consistent? "for" instead of "during"? "until" without "to"?
🎭 Role Play 2 — Monday Standup (Recycle listening)
You are David from the §3 audio. The manager (teacher) asks you the same kinds of questions — but now you answer with YOUR real data (not the audio's data). Add one extra invented detail.
David (Student)
Use David's structure: incomplete task + real reason + duration + next step. Mix regular and irregular verbs. Minimum 5 past-tense sentences.
Manager (Teacher)
"How long did you spend on it?" / "Why didn't you go?" / "When did you receive the last email?" / "What did you achieve last week?"
[Manager]: "Good morning David. Quick standup — what happened last week? I see several tasks still open."
Goal: the student recycles the audio's structure with their own data. Special note: does the student say "didn't go" (correct) or "didn't went" (S3 error)? Immediate correction if it appears.
Layer 1 · Pedagogical
  • RP1 uses REAL data. If the student doesn't know how to say their task in English → "Tell me in Spanish, we'll build it together, and then you repeat it on your own."
  • RP2 recycles the §3 audio with the student's own data — the student already knows the structure. Evaluate the spontaneity of irregular negatives in particular.
Layer 2 · Engagement
  • "You just gave your full Friday update in English — including the hard parts. That's more valuable than only reporting successes."
Layer 3 · Growth & Retention
  • Upsell: "S5 = Exit Task. You bring all of M1 together in a 3-minute report: regulars, irregulars, negatives, Wh-, duration. That's your exit qualification for level A2.1."
§ 06
Debate — Is honesty at work worth it?
8–10 min
🗣 Free Speaking — Debate with Negatives + Duration
"Valeria and David were completely transparent — they said exactly what they didn't do and why. Is that always the right strategy in professional life? Or is it sometimes better to say less?"
✅ Always be transparent
"I didn't finish it because… — and I worked on it for 4 hours."
"When I didn't reach the target, I explained the reason."
"My manager didn't fire me — they respected my honesty."
"I didn't achieve it, but I gave 100% effort."
⚠ Sometimes, say less
"I didn't mention the delay — it wasn't important yet."
"We didn't reach a decision, but I didn't explain why."
"Some managers don't want to hear 'I didn't finish.'"
"I worked on it all week — I didn't say it was incomplete."
I didn't finish because… I worked on it for… In my company, we didn't… Last week I didn't achieve… The manager didn't say anything about…
🎤 Optional Extension — Difficult Week Monologue · 2 minutes
"Describe your worst work week this year. What didn't you finish? How long did you work on it? What did you achieve? No notes — 2 minutes."
Minimum 6 sentences. At least 5 different negatives. At least 4 distinct duration expressions. At least 1 irregular negative. Close with next steps.
Teacher Follow-Up — After Debate
"When did YOU last give a 'difficult' update to your manager? What didn't you achieve? How long had you worked on it?"
"Did your company resolve the issue in the end? How long did it take from start to finish?"
"Think of a task you didn't finish last month. Give me the full professional explanation: didn't + duration + reason + next step."
"What's the longest you ever worked on something you still didn't finish? Use 'from…to' or 'for X hours'."
Layer 1 · Pedagogical
  • The debate activates negatives in authentic argumentative discourse. Rule: the student MUST use at least 4 negative sentences + 3 duration expressions during the debate. If they don't appear naturally: "Say the same thing with a past-tense example."
  • The teacher takes the opposite side. The follow-up questions simultaneously activate S3 (Wh-) + S4 (negative + duration).
Layer 2 · Engagement
  • "You just debated workplace culture in English using didn't, for, until, all afternoon. That's executive-level A2 English."
Layer 3 · Growth & Retention
  • Testimonial: "If your director asked you in English 'What didn't you finish this week and why?' — could you answer with concrete duration data? Yes. That's what you learned in S4."
§ SP
Speaking Practice
10–12 min
🗣 Guided Speaking — Friday Update, No Script
Your Real Week — Negatives + Duration, Free
No role cards. No script. Your teacher is your manager. Give your real Friday update — what got done, what didn't, and how long things took. Use didn't + base verb and at least two duration expressions.
Question 1
"Friday standup — what did you complete this week? Give me two specific things."
Hint: I completed… / I submitted… / I attended… and we decided… / I delivered…
Question 2
"What didn't you finish — and how long did you actually spend on it?"
Hint: I didn't finish… / I worked on it for… / I spent all morning on it but… / from… to… / I didn't complete it because…
Question 3
"Was there a meeting or call that didn't produce a result? What happened — and how long did it last?"
Hint: We had a meeting but we didn't… / The call lasted from… to… / We didn't agree on… / It lasted most of the afternoon…
Question 4
"What's the most honest thing you can tell me about this week — something that didn't go as planned?"
Hint: Honestly, I didn't… / The problem was that I didn't have enough time to… / I worked until… but I still didn't…
Question 5
"Ask me about MY week — one question with 'didn't' and one with 'how long.'"
Hint: Did you finish…? / What didn't you…? / How long did you work on…? / How long did the… last?
⏱ 10–12 min total 📋 5 questions 🎯 2+ negatives + 2+ duration 👥 Teacher as manager
⚡ Speaking Challenge — Closing
"Give me your real Friday update — 60 seconds, no notes. What did you finish, what didn't you finish, and how long did each thing take. At least 3 negatives and 2 duration expressions."
Teacher evaluates: didn't + BASE (not past tense) used correctly · at least 2 duration expressions (for / from-to / all morning / until / most of the day) · honest, specific content (not generic) · natural flow between completed and incomplete tasks. Compare to S4A §SP: are they producing didn't faster and with correct base form automatically? That automaticity is mastery.
Capa 1 · Pedagogical
  • S4B students have done a full Role Play (§5) with standup simulations and a Debate (§6). By this §SP all scaffolding is gone — they report as themselves, with their real week.
  • Stay in the "manager" frame for Q1–Q4. Don't soften the pressure: "And what didn't you finish? Be specific." Real managers ask this. The discomfort is the learning.
  • Q2 is the core: negative + duration together. Many students will give the negative but skip the duration ("I didn't finish it"). Push: "How long did you work on it?" Force the duration expression.
  • Q5 requires both a "didn't" question and a "how long" question — two different grammatical structures. If student only produces one, prompt: "Good — now ask me about duration too."
  • Speaking Challenge comparison: at S4A §SP, students needed 4-5 seconds to produce "I didn't finish." At S4B §SP it should come out in 1-2 seconds. That speed reduction is the mastery signal.
Capa 2 · Engagement
  • "That update you just gave me — if your manager called right now and asked 'How was your week?' you could answer in English. Without preparation. That's real."
  • After Speaking Challenge: "Count the 'didn't' forms. Each one you said correctly — that's a form that used to trip you up. Now it's automatic."
  • Q4 (honest answer): when a student gives a genuinely honest work update in English — acknowledge it: "That's a professional response. Honest and specific. That's what builds trust in a team."
Capa 3 · Growth & Retention
  • "S5 is the Module 1 exit. You'll combine everything — regular verbs, irregulars, negatives, Wh- questions, AND duration — in a 90-second spoken report. You already have all the pieces."
  • Upsell: "Module 2 opens with 'be going to' — future plans. Your standup will then cover last week AND next week. Same format, twice the power."
  • Testimonial trigger: "If you think about Session 1A — when you first tried to say 'I didn't finish' — how does that feel now compared to what you just did?"
