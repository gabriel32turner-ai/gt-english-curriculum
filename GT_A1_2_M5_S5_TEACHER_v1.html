T-S · Integrated
Mónica receives a phone call from a supplier: the delivery is delayed 3 days. She takes the message. Then she writes an internal email to her team with the information — using connectors and a Wh-question.
Phone: take the message. Email: subject + opening + body with connectors + question. Full M5 in one task.
Phone: "ConnectPro, Mónica speaking. … Thank you. I'll take note of that." Email — Subject: Delivery Delay Update. "Team, I'm writing to inform you that our supplier called this morning. Could you confirm when the new storage space is available? I'll keep you posted. Best regards, Mónica."
S-S · Role Play
Partner A is a manager. Partner B is an employee. Situation: B can't attend Friday's client presentation due to a medical appointment. B calls A first, then sends the formal absence email. A replies with 2 Wh-questions and offers to reschedule.
Call → Email → Reply. Full communication chain. 4 minutes total.
Call: "Hi, I'm afraid I can't attend Friday's presentation due to a medical appointment. I'm sorry for the short notice. Could you tell me who will cover for me?" Email: "Subject: Absence — Friday Presentation. Dear [Manager], I'm writing to inform you…" Reply: "Thank you for letting me know. Could you confirm who will present instead? And could you tell me when your appointment finishes — in case we can reschedule for later Friday?"
Class · Final Debate
Which communication channel is most important in your job: email, phone, or WhatsApp? Use at least 3 phrases from M5 in your answer. You have 45 seconds each.
Use: "I'm writing to…" / "Could you…?" / "First… Then…" / "I'll keep you posted" / "I'm afraid…"
"In my job, email is the most important because it creates a record. First, I use email for formal requests — 'Could you send us the delivery schedule?' Then, I use the phone when I need a quick answer. Finally, I use WhatsApp internally — but I'm afraid our international clients don't accept it for formal communication. I'll keep them posted by email."
⚡ TEACHER NOTE — Integrated Speaking ▼
Round 1 is the S5 diagnostic — it reveals who has internalized the full communication chain vs. who still compartmentalizes the skills. Walk the room during Round 2 pairs. Collect 2 complete email chains (call + email + reply) from different students and project them. Class evaluates: correct email type? Connectors present? Wh-question polite form? Round 3 is the emotional close — students articulate why English communication matters for their specific job. This is the "I can use this" moment.

M5 Grammar — Quick Reference

StructureSessionKey example

Object pronouns in commsS2Ame / him / her / it / us / them — "Send it to me."

Activity A — Error Correction: 5 Mistakes

Each sentence has one error from M5. Click the correct version.

1. "Please find attach the updated schedule."

✅ "Please find attached" — past participle, not noun or base verb.

2. "I'll transfer to you the Sales department."

✅ Object pronoun comes directly after the verb: "transfer you to." Not "transfer to you."

3. "Could you tell me when will arrive the delivery?"

✅ Inside "Could you tell me…" — no inversion. Subject before verb: "when the delivery will arrive."

4. "I'm sorry for inconvenience the."

✅ Fixed phrase: "I'm sorry for THE inconvenience." Always use the definite article here.

5. "The delay is because a transport problem."

✅ "Due to + noun" is the most professional form. "Because of + noun" also works — but "because + noun" alone (without "of") does not.

Activity B — Complete Email: Put in Order

This email uses all M5 structures. Drag the parts into the correct sequence.

  • ⠿Best regards, Mónica Herrera · Sales Manager · ConnectPro International · Tel: +52 33 1234 5678
  • ⠿Delivery Delay — Apology & Rescheduling Request
  • ⠿Could you confirm when the new delivery date works for your team? Also, could you tell me who is the contact person at your warehouse? I look forward to hearing from you. Don't hesitate to contact me if you have any questions.
  • ⠿Dear Ing. Torres, I'm writing to inform you that the delivery scheduled for Tuesday, June 3rd is delayed due to a transport problem in Guadalajara. I regret any inconvenience this causes.
  • ⠿First, the new estimated delivery date is Thursday, June 5th at 8 AM. Then, the driver will check in at Reception — Gate 3. Finally, please sign the delivery document and send us a copy by email. I'm sorry for the short notice.
★ GT ORIGINAL — The M5 Email in One View ▼
Once the drag activity is complete and the order confirmed, show the full email assembled on screen. Point out which structure comes from which session: Subject (S1A) → Opening with "I'm writing to…" (S1A) → "due to" (S4A) → "regret" (S4A) → "First/Then/Finally" (S3A) → "Could you…?" + Wh-question (S2A+S4A) → "I look forward to / Don't hesitate" (S3A) → Sign-off (S1A). This visual synthesis is the most powerful moment of S5 — students see their own knowledge assembled into a professional document. "This email — you could send this today."

Listening — Full M5 Integration
Mónica Herrera · ConnectPro · ~1 minute · All structures
10 min
M5 Integration — ConnectPro International, Guadalajara
A1.2 · M5 · S5 · Voice: Sarah (American English) · Speed: 0.80
ElevenLabs Script — ~175 words · ~65 seconds at 0.80 · Uses ALL M5 structures

Hello. My name is Mónica Herrera. I'm the Sales Manager at ConnectPro International in Guadalajara. Today I want to share a real situation from my week — it uses everything we practised in Module 5.

On Monday, I received a phone call from our supplier in Monterrey. I answered: "ConnectPro, Mónica speaking. How can I help you?" The supplier said the delivery was delayed. I took the message and said: "I'll keep you posted — thank you for letting me know."

Then I wrote an email to my team. Subject: Delivery Delay Update. "Dear team, I'm writing to inform you that our delivery is delayed due to a transport problem. First, please update the production schedule. Then, could you contact the warehouse to confirm the new arrival time? Finally, please send me a copy of the new schedule. I'm sorry for the inconvenience. I look forward to hearing from you. Best regards, Mónica."

Also, I sent a separate email to the supplier: "Could you tell me when the delivery will arrive? And could you confirm who the new contact person is? Please find attached our revised order form. Thank you in advance."

That is one Monday morning in international business. With Module 5, you can handle it.

Comprehension — True or False

1. Mónica received the call from a supplier in Guadalajara.

The supplier called from Monterrey, not Guadalajara.

2. In her team email, Mónica uses three connectors: First, Then, Finally.

✅ "First… Then… Finally…" — the complete S3A connector sequence.

3. Mónica asks the supplier two Wh-questions in the second email.

✅ "When will the delivery arrive?" + "Who is the new contact person?" — both polite Wh-questions.

4. Mónica closes the team email with "Best regards."

✅ "Best regards, Mónica" — correct professional sign-off.

5. In the supplier email, Mónica says "Thank you in advance."

✅ "Thank you in advance" — S3A closing phrase.
⚡ TEACHER NOTE — Listening Analysis ▼
Play once for gist. Second listen: students track which M5 structure they hear — they raise a hand when they identify one. Count them together: subject line, phone greeting, "I'll keep you posted," connectors, polite imperatives, "due to," Wh-question, "please find attached," "thank you in advance." That's 9+ M5 structures in ~175 words. After the T/F: "This is what you can do after Module 5. That Monday morning — you can handle it." The last sentence of the script is the emotional close of the listening and of the module.

★ GT ORIGINAL — Running the Exit Task ▼
Part 1 (phone call): Run as a live role play with one volunteer. Teacher is the caller. 90 seconds max. Part 2 (email): 5-minute individual writing sprint. After the timer: 2-3 students read their emails aloud. Teacher scores live using the rubric — click on screen with students watching. This transparency is powerful: students see exactly where they are and what "Exceeding" looks like. Score of 15-18 = Module 5 complete at A1.2 level. Score below 12 = recommend S4B/S5 review before M6. Certificate below triggers on "generate."
