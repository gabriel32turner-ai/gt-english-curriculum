§ 01
Picture Prompt — ¿Verdad o mentira?
🖼️ Choose a photo · The teacher makes ONE false statement about the people · The student corrects with "No, she isn't — she's actually very ___. Her ___ is ___." · From the very first second!
⏱ 5–7 MIN
🚫 S4B — NO new grammar · NO readings · 100% oral production · to be neg + short answers + his/her/their from S4A recycled in correction and free production · LAST B session of M3
👀 Choose a photo. Look at the people. The teacher is going to say something FALSE about them — you have to correct with "No, she/he isn't… She/He's actually very ___. Her/His ___ is ___." Combine negation + adj + his/her!
⚡ Quick Win — 20 seconds
Niega esta frase en inglés: "He is my manager." → "No, he isn't! He is my colleague." ¡La negación con to be desde S4A!

Layer 1 · Pedagogical
  • ACTION: Make ONE clearly false statement about the chosen photo. The student must correct with "No, she isn't + correct adj + his/her." Minimum 3 correction sentences.
  • Verify that the student uses: (1) negative short answer, (2) correct positive adj, (3) his/her/their in the correction sentence.
  • Variation: the student makes ONE false statement → the teacher corrects. Switch roles. Activates spontaneous production.
Layer 2 · Engagement
  • "Active correction is harder than making a statement — you need to deny, produce adj and use his/her simultaneously. That is exactly S4A in action." 🔥
Layer 3 · Retention
  • "In §5 the 'Correct Me!' game does exactly this — 10 rounds with false statements about your real team. This picture prompt is the warm-up."

§ 03
Audio Model — Ana's Team Q&A
🎧 Ana answers questions about her team with to be neg + short answers + his/her · Shadowing 3 phrases · Then you answer the same about your team
⏱ 8–10 MIN
🎙️ Ana García — Team Q&A with to be neg + his/her/their
ElevenLabs · speed 0.78 · Escucha completo primero · Luego shadowing ↓

🔊 Escucha → repite → escucha. Pon atención a "No, he/she isn't" + adj + his/her en cada respuesta. 3 rondas.

🔊 Shadowing — Las 3 Frases Modelo de S4A
Each phrase has: correction with isn't + positive adj + his/her. All 3 elements together. Say all 3 without stopping.
1
"Is he boring? No, he isn't! He's very funny. His ideas are always creative."
2
"Is she impatient? No, she isn't — she's very patient. Her office is always perfect."
3
"Are they boring? No, they aren't — they're very hardworking. Their work is excellent."
🎙️ Now You — Mismo Formato que Ana
Answer 3 questions about your real team — just like Ana. Use isn't/aren't + positive adj + his/her/their. Without notes.
➡ "Is your manager impatient? — No, he/she isn't! He/She is very ___. His/Her ___ is always ___." · "Are they hardworking? — Yes, they are! Their ___ is always ___."
Layer 1 · Pedagogical
  • ACTION: Shadowing — verify the 3 elements in each phrase: (1) negative short answer, (2) positive adj, (3) his/her/their. If one is missing → repeat the phrase.
  • "Now You": the teacher asks questions about the student's real team with deliberately false statements: "Is your manager shy?" → student corrects.
  • The 3 shadowing phrases = exact model for the §5 game. The student who memorizes them → §5 will flow naturally.
Layer 2 · Engagement
  • "You just defended your team in English — you denied false statements and corrected them with adj and his/her. That is what professionals do in international meetings." 💼
Layer 3 · Retention
  • "§4 True or False is the rehearsal. §5 Correct Me! is the game. You already have the model — now it is real speed."
§ 04
Role Play — "True or False about my team"
🎭 3 progressive rounds · The teacher makes statements about the student's team · The student confirms (Yes, she is!) or corrects (No, she isn't — she's actually ___)
⏱ 12–15 MIN
🎭 Round 1 — Guiada (scaffold visible)
❓ "Is this true about your team?"
The teacher makes statements about the student's team — some true, some false. The student confirms or corrects using short answer + adj + his/her. Scaffold visible. 6 statements.
👔 Maestro — afirmaciones
Afirmaciones mezcladas (T/F)
Make 6 statements about the student's real team — 3 true, 3 false. Mix real names with incorrect adj. The student does not know which are T or F until they speak.
🗣️ Alumno
Tu equipo real
Confirm: "Yes, she is! Her ___ is always ___." · Correct: "No, he isn't ___. He's actually very ___. His ___ is ___." · Scaffold visible.
Scaffold Round 1:
✅ TRUE: "Yes, she is! She's very ___. Her ___ is always ___."
❌ FALSE: "No, he isn't ___. He's actually very ___. His ___ is ___."
👥 PLURAL: "Yes, they are!" / "No, they aren't ___. They're ___. Their ___ is ___."
🎭 Round 2 — Semi-libre (without scaffold · más rápido)
❓ Speed Round — 10 segundos para responder
Same statements but faster — 10 seconds to answer. Without scaffold. The teacher adds direct questions: "Is she ___?" → immediate short answer + 1 extension sentence.
👔 Maestro — speed prompts
Preguntas disponibles
"Is your manager patient?" · "Is she shy?" · "Are your colleagues hardworking?" · "Is your supervisor boring?" · "Is he organized?" · "Are they friendly?" · "Whose project is the most important?"
🗣️ Alumno — libre
Without scaffold
Short answer + correction/confirmation + his/her/their. "Yes, she is — very! Her ___ is always ___." / "No, he isn't ___. His ___ is ___."
⭐ Round 3 — M3 Full Combo · 90 segundos
🎤 Full Team Description — M3 completo without notes
90 seconds. Describe your full team using ALL of M3: intro formula + adj + 's + to be neg + his/her/their + short answer. Without the teacher as interlocutor. At least 3 people.
🏆 3 intro formulas · adj+very/quite · 's + his/her · isn't/aren't · short answers = ⭐ M3 Full Combo · 2 intro · adj · isn't = ✅ Good · fewer = 📋 Practice M3 combo at home
Layer 1 · Pedagogical
  • ACTION: For the false statements in Round 1 — use negative adjectives (boring, impatient, shy, disorganized). The student corrects them with the S1A positives.
  • Round 3: verify that all 4 M3 elements appear: intro (S3A) + adj+very/quite (S1A) + 's/his/her (S2A+S4A) + isn't/short answer (S4A). If any is missing → note for the S5 Exit Task.
  • Round 3 is the general rehearsal for the S5 Exit Task. Evaluate silently and give specific feedback at the end.
Layer 2 · Engagement
  • "In Round 3 you did the M3 Full Combo — 4 sessions, 90 seconds, without notes. That is already 80% of the S5 Exit Task." 🏆
Layer 3 · Retention
  • "§5 Correct Me! is the fastest and most competitive game in S4B — 10 statements, how many can you correct correctly in 30 seconds each?"

§ 06
Debate — ¿Es de mala educación corregir?
💬 Free oral production · 3 questions · Use to be neg + adj + his/her/their to defend your position · No correct answer — just real English
⏱ 8–10 MIN
💬 Pregunta 1 — Corrección en el trabajo
Is it rude to correct someone's description of you in English? For example, if your manager says "This is David — he isn't very organized" and it's wrong — what do you do?
"In my culture, I think it's OK / not OK to say 'Actually, that isn't quite right. I AM very ___.' because ___. His/Her reputation is important because ___."
🟢 Yes, correct! "Actually, I'm very organized!" — Direct and professional
🔴 No, stay quiet. "His intention isn't bad — it isn't worth correcting." — Polite and pragmatic
💬 Pregunta 2 — True or False sobre ti
I'm going to make 3 statements about you as a professional. You tell me: TRUE or FALSE — and if false, correct me!
Teacher: "You're quite shy in meetings." · "Your work isn't very organized." · "You aren't very creative." — Student corrects or confirms with full M3 sentence.
⭐ Pregunta 3 — M3 Grand Conversation
Tell me about YOUR perfect team — real or imaginary. Use ALL of M3: intro + adj + 's + isn't + his/her + short answer. 60 seconds. No notes. Go!
"My perfect team has ___. [Name]'s role is ___. She isn't ___ — she's very ___. Her ___ is ___. Is she creative? Yes, she absolutely is!" — M3 Full Combo libre.
Layer 1 · Pedagogical
  • ACTION: Question 2 — make deliberately FALSE statements to provoke spontaneous correction. This is the most natural activation of to be neg in all of S4B.
  • Question 3 (Grand Conversation): 60-second timer. Evaluate: do all 4 M3 elements appear? Note for the final module feedback in S5.
  • If the student says "I'm not boring" → celebrate: "You just used 'I'm not' correctly! That is exactly S4A." Micro-celebration increases retention.
Layer 2 · Engagement
  • "In Question 3 you described your ideal team — with full M3, in 60 seconds, in English. That is not a language exercise. It is communicative leadership." 🔥
Layer 3 · Retention & Upsell
  • "S5 is the Review and final Exit Task. If Question 3 flowed smoothly → the Exit Task will shine. If not → S5 has the necessary drills to consolidate it."

🗣️ 3 Frases para Esta Semana
Tres frases modelo para usar negación y pronombres en inglés. Úsalas esta semana. 💬
1
"No, he isn't my manager — he's my colleague."
No, él no es mi manager — es mi compañero.
✅ Negación con to be: isn't = is not. Muy natural en conversación.
2
"Her office isn't on this floor — it's on the third floor."
Su oficina no está en este piso — está en el tercer piso.
💼 Her + noun + isn't. Combina pronombres posesivos con negación.
3
"They aren't free today — their team is very busy."
No están libres hoy — su equipo está muy ocupado.
🏆 Their + noun para grupos. aren't = are not. Patrón clave para hablar de equipos.
